# Feedback: Sam (29 Oct 2026, release 47)
**Before I started:** The brief was clear and I was comfortable with it. Where the app asked something the brief didn't cover, I made it up the way I would: two 60 × 20 cm troughs (herbs, salad), a 25 cm chilli pot, a 35 cm strawberry tower with 8 plants, and the tomato left off because it's on its way out. I picked the "Just a list" option because it said "Simplest", and then tried the plan view once to see whether it knew about shade. The garden name box on step 1 didn't take what I typed (the app called it "My balcony" anyway, so I left it).

I'm Sam, 29, a nurse doing long shifts. I've got a 1.2 × 3 m north-east balcony on the fourth floor in Hackney, everything's in pots, and I pay for Planta for my 40 houseplants. I used the app on a phone-sized screen (390 px) in three goes, about 75 minutes in all: setup and adding my containers, then asking it my questions and checking Today and the shopping list, then coming back "the next day". I took 56 screenshots, saved in `scratchpad/testers/sam/`.

I'd recommend it to a friend: 3/10 (for a friend with a balcony; maybe 6 for one with an allotment).

## Would I pay for it?
**No, not as it is.** If it had a real balcony mode, I'd pay **£10–15 a year**, or **£15 one-off**. That's well under Planta's £34.99, because it would be a second app for about 10 outdoor pots, not my main one. Before I paid, it would have to:
- know my balcony faces north-east and gets about 3 hours of sun, and plan around that;
- tell me when to water pots that the rain doesn't reach, with wind counted in;
- stop giving me greenhouse, cold-frame, bare-root-tree and leaf-mould jobs.

I'd cancel the first time it said "nothing needs watering" while my troughs were bone dry after a windy week.

## Would I keep using it?
Next week? Maybe once, on a day off, to look at the winter purslane job. A month? No. Today keeps asking me to sow things "in the shed", put them "in a cold frame" or "greenhouse border", and "gather fallen leaves to make leaf mould". None of that exists on a fourth-floor balcony, so I'd learn to skip most of the list, and then I'd stop opening it. The one thing that would bring me back, a water nudge on a dry windy day, only shows up in the plan view, and there it got it wrong. Planta already pings me about watering, and that's the habit this app would need to beat.

## What I like (keep it)
- **Setup step 3 has a "Balcony" choice** ("Pots and a trough along the railing"). It's the first garden app that's even asked. It was the right first impression.
- **Postcode search** found Hackney straight away, with a clear line about what's sent where.
- **Compost in litres** on the shopping list ("Multi-purpose compost, about 150 litres"), and each container says what it holds ("60 cm long and 20 cm wide, 30 cm deep: about 36 litres of compost"). That's exactly the unit I buy in.
- **Pot-size warnings** are genuinely useful: "Chilli pepper wants a pot about 30 cm across or more; this chilli pot is 25 cm (about 15 litres)… dries out fast. Pot it on." That's the advice I didn't know I needed for the tomato that struggled.
- **"Fill the bed"** put 4 winter purslane in a 60 cm trough, so it answers "how much fits" in one tap. I'd want to see the number before I commit, but it's good.
- **The chilli plant page** says "Plants can be kept indoors over winter and cropped again" and has a "compact, for a windowsill" type. That's real balcony-and-flat advice.
- **The How to sheet** for sowing lists what you need and offers "a light windowsill", not just a propagator. That's short and clear.
- **Recording a harvest** with "A handful / A bowl / A basket" is one tap, and the job ticks itself off.
- **Plant search with the Part shade filter** turned up corn salad, winter purslane, rocket, pak choi, sorrel and alpine strawberry. That's a genuinely good shade-salad list, once I'd found it.
- **Keeping it on the device, with no account.** I like that.

## What I don't like
Ranked, worst first.
1. **It never asks which way the balcony faces or how much sun it gets.** Setup asks for my place, the type of space and a kit. Nothing about aspect, the floor above, wind or hours of sun. I found the "How sunny is it?" setting only by digging into each container's ··· menu. Every container defaults to "Not sure", so the kit's basil, thyme and chives looked fine until I set the shade myself.
2. **Watering is wrong for a balcony, and hidden.** Water advice only appears in plan view, under ··· → Show → water. It said "Nothing likely to need watering this week. 4 mm of rain in the three days to then." My pots sit under the balcony above and the wind dries them. Rain is close to irrelevant. In list view there's no watering at all, which is the thing I most wanted (screenshot `50-water.png`).
3. **Today is full of garden jobs.** Sowing sweet peas "in a cold frame", purslane "into a greenhouse border or cold frame", and then "Gather fallen leaves to make leaf mould", "Plant bare-root trees, shrubs and roses", "Clean and oil your tools", "Open the greenhouse vents", "Keep the cold frame lid open". I told it Balcony, so it knows (`33-today.png`).
4. **The kit fought itself.** I chose "Kitchen herbs", and straight away the app warned me about its own choices:
   - Pot 2: "Rosemary wants a pot about 45 cm across or more; this pot 2 is 30 cm";
   - "Mint and rosemary are 608 mm apart; they need about 750 mm". They're in separate pots!
   - the 2 m planter it made was longer than any trough I'd fit on a 3 m balcony with a door;
   - basil, thyme and chives were picked for a balcony that, for most people in a flat, is shady (`11-garden.png`, `48-details.png`).
5. **A wall of orange warnings.** One 60 cm herb trough gave six warnings, and Today says "19 things to check in your planting". None of them said what to grow instead. It told me basil hates part shade but never said "try parsley, mint, chives or lemon balm instead" (`15-about-trough.png`).
6. **"Add a plant" doesn't filter for the container.** In my 60 × 20 cm part-shade Salad trough, "Sow or plant now" offered a butterfly bush, wisteria, a Japanese maple, lilac, camellia and forsythia (`27b-add-plant-top.png`).
7. **Sizes in metres.** A pot is "Across … m", so I typed 25 for a 25 cm pot and it happily made a "Pot · 25 m across" without a question (`29-pot-25.png`). Nobody measures a pot in metres.
8. **Plan-speak in the list view.**
   - "Basil is outside Herb trough" (I'd only made the trough smaller).
   - "There wasn't room inside your garden's outline, so it's set just outside it" (what outline?).
   - The "Plants in pots" help tells me to "drag it where it stands" and "zoom in" (`14-after-trough.png`, `25-added.png`).
9. **The tab is called "Shed"** and jobs say "Sow in the shed". I don't have one, and nor does anyone in my building. "Windowsill" or "Seedlings" would do.
10. **The weekly reminder is Monday only**, with no choice of day or time. I work Mondays half the time. Planta reminds me per plant, on the day it's due.

## Contradictions
- **Strawberry tower:** "Too crowded: … want a pot about 42 cm across between them; this strawberry tower is 35 cm" and "its plants want about 590% of the room it has". 42 cm against 35 cm is about 144%, not 590%. The toast said "It's full now" at the same moment (`32-tower.png`).
- **Things to check:** Today said "19 things to check in your planting"; the plan view said "6 things to check" for the same garden at the same time. The shade warnings disappeared in the plan, because it "Assumes flat ground" and full sun. Switching to the plan quietly overrode my "part shade" (`48-details.png`).
- **Kit against kit:** the kit put rosemary in a 30 cm pot, then warned it needs 45 cm. It put mint and rosemary in separate pots, then warned they're too close together.
- **Chilli:** the plant page says "Tender: keep above 10°C", but the Tuck-in job's How to gives fleece and pegs.
- **Strawberry spacing:** the plant list says "40 cm apart", but the page says "In a bed: 300 mm each way".
- **Plantings count:** "6 beds and areas · 16 plantings". My 8 strawberry plants in one tower count as 8 plantings and are listed as 8 separate rows under the tower.
- **Shopping list** still lists "Strawberry, on your Want to grow list" after I'd put 8 everbearers in the tower.

## What's superfluous
For a balcony, I'd hide all of these:
- the soil picker (clay, loam, sandy, chalky, peaty);
- weeding reminders;
- "Next year, 2027, with the crops rotated" (crop rotation across three pots?);
- the bed kinds "A greenhouse", "A cold frame", "A fruit cage", "A patch of ground" and "Cardboard over weeds";
- the "Around the garden" jobs;
- the 3D view, "Play the year" and Measure.

I'd also cut these:
- **The welcome-back screen.** It has five buttons ("Enter my garden", "See my tasks", "About my garden", "What's new", "Continue to Today"), and three of them do nearly the same thing. Just open on Today.
- **The big pumpkin photo** at the top of Today and Garden. It takes a third of the phone screen on every visit.
- **"Garden journal", "Put it on your home screen" and "Getting started 3 of 4"** cards stacked at the bottom of Today. Show them once, then get them out of the way.
- **Recipes on every plant page** (Eton mess, chilli jam). They're nice, but they're not why I'm here.
- **"Inspire me" budget and time chips.** Daffodils for a salad trough isn't inspiration.

## What's missing
- An aspect question in setup (north, north-east and so on), plus "covered by a balcony above?" and "windy?", applied to every container.
- **Watering reminders for pots**, per container, that count wind, heat and no rain. This is the core of why I'd use it.
- **"What grows here?"** for a container: show me plants that suit its light and size, ranked. The Part shade filter exists, but it isn't linked from the warnings.
- **Container types:** grow bag, hanging basket, strawberry tower or vertical planter, railing planter. Sizes in cm or litres, with presets like "60 cm trough" and "30 cm pot".
- **Weight:** what a filled trough weighs wet. My letting agent limits the load, and 150 litres of compost is roughly 4 bags, about 80 kg, up four flights. The shopping list should say bags and kg, and maybe suggest peat-free lightweight compost.
- **Plant ID or "what's wrong with it?" from a photo.** Planta has it, and I use it.
- **A balcony winter list:** what I can grow or do now (winter salad, garlic in a pot, pansies and violas, bulbs in a trough, bringing the chilli in).
- **Houseplants**, or at least a link to them. No monstera or pothos. Fine if it's outdoors-only, but say so.
- A reminder day and time I can choose.
- An urgent frost nudge. The Shed says "First frost about 2 Nov", four days away, but Today's chilli job doesn't say "this week".

## My top five changes
1. **Make setup ask about the balcony.** On "Balcony", add questions for facing direction, a roof or balcony above, wind, and rough hours of sun. Set every container's light from the answers, and say so ("3 h of morning sun: part shade").
2. **Water reminders for containers**, shown on Today in list view as well as the plan. Treat covered containers as getting no rain, bring the reminder forward on windy or warm days, and size it per pot ("the 25 cm chilli pot dries fastest").
3. **A Balcony filter on jobs, kits and settings.** Hide greenhouse, cold-frame, bare-root, leaf-mould, weeding, soil and crop rotation. Swap in pot versions ("sow straight into the trough", "bring the chilli indoors below 10 °C", "feed per 10 litres"). Give a "Shady balcony" kit (parsley, mint, chives, rocket, winter purslane, alpine strawberry, violas).
4. **Turn warnings into answers.** Group one container's warnings into a single line, and attach "Grow this instead" with three plants that suit its light and size. Filter "Add a plant" by the container's light and size, so a 60 cm trough never offers wisteria. Fix the plant-list count bug, so "22 shade plants" shows 22.
5. **Container sizes in cm or litres, with presets** (60 cm trough, 30 cm pot, grow bag, hanging basket, strawberry tower). Range-check the numbers. Show the wet weight and the number of compost bags, so I know what I'm carrying up four flights and whether the balcony can take it.
