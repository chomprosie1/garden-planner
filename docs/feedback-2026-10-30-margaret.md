## The worst thing: it told me to pull up my parsnips
I added parsnips, sown in April, as "Already in the ground", Harvesting. On 30 October Today's first job was:

> **Clear and compost: Parsnip in Bed C.** Its season's over. Pull it up for the compost heap, and the ground's free for what's next. Ticking this takes it off your plan.

The card says "Harvest Oct–Mar" and also "Its season's over: time to clear it". The Timeline says "Finished about 31 Oct". The draft for next year says "Parsnip and leek, late are still standing until about April". It stays on Today on 10 December, 20 January and 10 March. Parsnips are at their best after a frost. A beginner would compost a whole row. Last time it was "Finished by about 10 Dec" and I said I'd lose half of them. This is worse: it's now an instruction, it's the first thing on the page, and ticking it takes them off my plan.

## What I like (keep it)
- **Today only lists what's left to do.** With everything entered, Now was two jobs. No Sow, no Plant, and no chips on the beds for what I'd told it was in.
- **"6 ft" and "8ft" work.** My greenhouse is 2.44 × 1.83 m.
- **Extra large on the first screen**, before anything else. Settings' drop-downs grow with it (63 px).
- **The fruit cage holds my fruit.** Eight raspberries, three blackcurrants and two gooseberries fit, "Nearly full".
- **Sweet peas in the cold frame** have no spacing warning, and it's still plant out 1 April, flower 6 June.
- **Resting.** The fruit reads Resting in December and January, and Growing by March, without my doing anything.
- **Winter pruning.** December and January: "Cut up to a third of the oldest, darkest stems out at the base" for blackcurrants. Gooseberries are there by March. I'd want them in February, as its own text says.
- **Around the garden** now has "Cut summer raspberry canes that have fruited down to the ground, and tie in the new ones", the greenhouse glass and the frame lid.
- **White rot** now reads the same on the garlic and onion pages: "don't grow onions, garlic or leeks there again: it lasts in the soil for 15 years or more. Rotating crops doesn't clear it." Right.
- **The overwintering onion page** has one story now: Hardy, Plant Sep–Nov, Harvest May–Jul, and "Push them in so just the tip shows". The claim about bolting has gone.
- **Next year's draft:** "Taken until about August by garlic and onion, overwintering", and it follows the beans with brassicas.
- **"I've done this"** on a How to, with a separate Close.
- **The plan on the iPad** now fits the whole garden, with everything inside the boundary.

## What I don't like
Worst first.
1. **The parsnips**, above.
2. **Adding a bed renames the ones I have.** The kit has Bed 1 and Bed 2. When I added beds and called them Bed 1 and Bed 2, the app changed mine to "Bed 1 1" and "Bed 2 1", and the new ones to "Bed 1 2" and "Bed 2 2". I complained about renaming last time, when it was "Another like this". It should say the name's taken, or leave mine alone.
3. **Late leeks disagree with themselves.** The card says "Harvest Dec–Apr" and "Probably ready to pick now" on 30 October, and the printed sheet says "Harvesting about 24 Oct". Mine are ready from November, so the words are nearer than the "Dec". They can't all be right.
4. **Blackcurrants flower "about 4 Jun 2027".** They flower in April here. The harvest is then 5 July, which is about right.
5. **Raspberries and broad beans are still early.** Raspberry flowers 3 May and fruit 4 June; here it's late May and late June. Broad beans sown in October crop "about 3 May"; here it's late May at the earliest.
6. **The drop-downs in Next year are still small:** 36 px tall at Extra large.
7. **The onion's Keeping it line:** "hang in nets or plaits somewhere cool and airy for months. Overwintering onions don't keep like this". It's now true, but why say the first part on this page at all?
8. **The print-out repeats itself.** Each blackcurrant has its own long line, and dates that have passed appear with no year ("Growing about 5 Aug" beside "Planted 1 Oct 2025"). I'd want one line for the three bushes, and the year ahead only.
