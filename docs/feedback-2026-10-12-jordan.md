# Feedback: Jordan (12 Oct 2026, release 36)
I'm Jordan, 27, in a rented terrace in Leeds (LS6 3AA): a lawn about 4 × 5 m, fences on two sides, paving by the back door, no beds yet, no gardening knowledge. I used the app on a phone-sized screen (390 × 844) for roughly an hour and a half. I set up from scratch with the **drawn plan**. I added, moved, resized and hand-drew beds and a patio, picked the "Bulbs and autumn veg" kit plus strawberries, tomatoes and potatoes, and worked through every job on Today and its "How to". I also tried the glossary, search, help, the plant pages, the shopping list, 3D, the share picture, "Ideas for the lawn" and Settings. Screenshots are in the session scratchpad under `jordan/` (file names below).

I'd recommend it to a friend: 6/10. To a friend who already has beds I'd say 7; to one with only a lawn, 5.

## Would I keep using it?
In a week: yes. I've got garlic and onions to plant and bulbs to put in, Today tells me exactly that, and the How-to sheets mean I don't need YouTube for most of it. Ticking a job off feels good, though nothing celebrates it. In a month, probably not daily. Once October's jobs are done, Today will be mostly "clean and oil your tools" and dahlia jobs I don't have. My real problem, "I have a lawn, how do I make a bed?", is never raised by the app; I only found the answer (Cardboard over weeds) by browsing all of help. If Today had walked me through making the beds first, I'd be hooked. ChatGPT would have told me to do that first.

## What I like (keep it)
- **The How-to sheet on every job** (Today → How to). "What you need", four steps, "About 15 minutes a row", then Done, which ticks the job. That's exactly the format I wanted last time. The broad-bean "sow outside" one even explains "a watering can with a rose (the sprinkler head)".
- **The glossary** (dotted words, and Help → Garden words). The two-line definitions are excellent: "Module tray", "Sets", "Hardening off", "Overwintering". The pop-up (46-gloss.png) is fast and gets out of the way. This fixes most of my 35 words.
- **Kits sized for a first garden.** It was 84 bulbs this time, not 600, with an obvious "Make it bigger" (08a-step4-top.png). The kit was right for October: everything in it can actually go in now.
- **The shopping list uses real words**: "Onion sets, overwintering, about 25", "Seed potatoes", "Crocus bulbs, 24 bulbs". It also explains seed compost ("finer, and lower in feed, than multi-purpose").
- **Today's layout.** The photo is now a slim header, "Now · 0 of 8 done this month" sits up top, and the jobs are grouped by Sow / Plant / Weeding. I could see three jobs without scrolling.
- **Setup's four steps** with the postcode lookup ("LS6 3AA, Leeds" on Enter). It's quick, and the privacy line is reassuring.
- **Strawberry and tomato pages.** "Not now: sow under cover from February, then plant out from May" answers my first question straight away. The pests, recipes and "pick fully red fruit with the stalk" detail are where it beats Googling.
- **Plant in a bed from a plant page** (About → Plant in a bed → tap the bed). Three taps, and it then gives a quantity on the shopping list.
- **The Shed's empty state** (53-shed.png): a picture, one sentence, one button. That's how empty states should look.
- **Context help** from the ? on each tab. "This week's jobs" explains Today in four lines.

## What I don't like
Worst first.

1. **The app never helps a garden with no beds.** I chose "Garden: a lawn, a border and raised beds" at 4 × 7 m. I got a border and a lawn but **no raised beds**, so the kit's promise ("garlic and onion sets in one bed, broad beans in the other") turned into "on your Want to grow list" (09-landing.png). Nothing on Today said "you need somewhere to plant these: here's how to make a bed in a lawn". Searching "dig up lawn" found nothing. The answer exists (Help → Cardboard over weeds), but I only found it by reading the whole help index. That's the first job of any first garden.
2. **Two broad-bean jobs with no "pick one".** Today shows "Sow under cover: Broad bean" and "Sow outside: Broad bean" one after the other. As a beginner I read that as two jobs. It needs to say "Either… or…", or ask me once.
3. **How-tos say "at the spacing below", but the spacing isn't below.** This happens for garlic and onion (50-garlic-howto.png). "For this garlic" gives depth and feed, not how far apart, so I had to open the plant page to find "100 mm". It's the single most important number for planting.
4. **Generic How-to steps give wrong-feeling advice for this crop.** The under-cover broad-bean sheet says "keep it warm" and lists a propagator, while the job itself says "kept in a cold frame". The sow-outside sheet says "sow the seeds thinly along it" for big broad-bean seeds that go in one by one. I'd follow the steps literally.
5. **Drawing: dragging often pans instead of moving.**
   - When a small bed is selected, the four "1.20 m" labels cover it entirely, so grabbing it drags the whole plan (14-move-bed.png, 17-move-bed2.png). Moving only worked when the bed wasn't selected.
   - A new patio is "set" (locked) as soon as you tap away, so the next drag pans too. "Unlock" is in a floating pill.
   - Tip 2, "The lock", talks about a lock button that doesn't exist in the simple toolbar; it appears only under ··· → Advanced tools.
6. **New things drop in odd places.** The patio landed half outside my garden (23-patio.png). The bed landed at the bottom of the lawn, right by the house. A tray item couldn't be dragged onto the plan; only a tap placed it.
7. **Floating panels cover what I'm working on.** Selecting the garlic row gave two stacked panels ("One / A row / Fill the bed" and "Mark as sown − 20 +") on top of the bed (44-garlic-filled.png). With Advanced on, the bed panel is wider than the phone, so Delete wraps to a second line and the plan slides sideways, showing the photo behind it (40-dup.png, 27-unlocked.png).
8. **Search doesn't understand plurals or questions.** "strawberries", "tomatoes" and "potatoes" don't find the plants, only glossary entries. "strawberry" puts "Add a tree: Strawberry tree" first. "what can I grow now" returns only the help page "Adding plants"; "how do I start" returns "Truss" and "Seedbed". Every app I use handles plurals.
9. **"About" throws me out of Today.** Tapping About on a job opens the Plants tab with a back link to "All plants", not back to my jobs (69-onion-about.png).
10. **3D still opens on the house roof.** "From above" changed nothing, and only a manual drag showed the beds (61-3d.png, 63-3d-turn.png). The lawn is grey, with a big translucent slab behind.
11. **The share picture isn't shareable** (64-share.png). Half of it is the house, the right third is empty grid, and the labels are too small to read. I'd never post it.
12. **Settings is a wall.** It has climate averages, "Degrees clockwise from the top of your plan to north", pH, "About 300 days above 5 °C" and a backup warning that "this browser hasn't promised to keep your garden". It's scary and mostly irrelevant to me.
13. **Inconsistent names for the same thing.**
    - The list is called "Want to grow list" (Today), "sowing list" (Ideas), and "your list to grow" (setup).
    - Spacing is in mm on plant pages but depth is in cm in jobs.
    - "Mark as sown" appears on garlic, which is planted.
    - "Shed" is a tab, a How-to link ("Sow in the shed"), and also a building you can put on the plan. I don't have a shed.

## What's superfluous
- **Latin names and Latin family names** on the plant list and pages: move them under "More".
- **"Make my own version"** and **"Add your own plant"** (the latter is the biggest button on the Plants tab). They're for experts, so hide them.
- **"500 of 604 plants" and "Sow or plant now 171."** That's far too many for me. Lead with "Easy to start (16)" by default.
- **Generic "Around the garden" jobs** that don't apply: "Lift and store dahlia tubers" (no dahlias), and "Plant garlic cloves" after I'd ticked garlic. They make Today look like homework.
- **Settings' climate block** (July days, January nights, growing season, stations), and the north-degrees field. Collapse them under "Advanced".
- **3D, Play the year, the year slider and timelapse** on a phone, for a 4 × 7 m garden. That's three ways to see time passing, and none told me anything Today didn't.
- **Show: sun hours / shade / in flower / harvest / water** in the top-right dropdown. It's always on screen and I never needed it.
- **The share card** as it stands. It's not worth a button until it looks good.
- **"Garden journal" and "Put it on your home screen" cards** at the foot of Today, every visit. Show them once.
- **Feeds on the shopping list.** That's five for a first garden: blood, fish and bone; bonemeal; chicken manure pellets; liquid tomato feed; well-rotted manure. A beginner needs one general feed and some compost. Liquid tomato feed "for daffodil, tulip" baffled me.

## What's missing
- **A "first, make your beds" path** for lawn-only gardens: Today's first job should be "Make a bed: cardboard over the lawn, this weekend", with a How-to and a shopping line (cardboard, compost, how much).
- **Spacing in every planting How-to**, plus "how many to buy" for everything on the shopping list.
- **"Buy now" vs "buy later"** on the shopping list (tomato seed and seed potatoes aren't needed until February or March), plus a line on where (garden centre, online, pound shop for pots).
- **Pictures of tools** (trowel, dibber, hoe, hand fork) in "What you need".
- **A friendly "what can I grow now?" answer** in search, and plural matching.
- **A first-frost heads-up on Today.** The Shed says first frost is about 22 Oct, ten days away, but Today never mentions it.
- **Naming beds when they're created.** I have two beds both called "Bed", and jobs say "Garlic in Bed".
- **A bit of reward when a job's done**: a streak, "3 of 8 done" with a little moment. That's the Duolingo bit that keeps me coming back.

## My top five changes
1. **Give lawn-only gardens a "make your first bed" start.** If the kit's beds don't fit, or there are none, make the first Today job "Make a bed on the lawn (cardboard method)", with a How-to and a shopping line, and still place the kit's crops in it. Never leave a kit's veg as "on your Want to grow list".
2. **Put crop-specific spacing (and count) in every planting and sowing How-to**, and replace the generic steps where they're wrong for the crop. For example, broad beans are sown one by one, 20 cm apart, and autumn ones sit in a cold frame, not somewhere warm.
3. **Merge "sow under cover" and "sow outside" for the same planting into one job with "Either… or…"** (or a one-tap choice), so a beginner sees one thing to do.
4. **Make the plan behave like a phone app.** A press on a selected item moves it (hide the size labels while dragging, or move them outside the shape). Don't auto-lock ground. Place new items inside the boundary and clear of others. Keep the selection panel within the screen and off the selected item.
5. **Make search forgiving.** Match plurals ("tomatoes"), rank plants above actions, and answer "what can I grow now" with the "Sow or plant now / Easy to start" list.
