# Feedback: Callum (30 Oct 2026, release 57a, interim round)
**Before I started:** The brief was clear. I tested 0.57.1 at 1366 × 768 in a browser. I entered the garden as a list, for speed, and looked at the plan afterwards, so I didn't redraw it or plant with the mouse this time. I entered height (250 m) and frost hollow, then my own frost dates (5 June and 5 September). My dates below are with those typed in. For the rotation I gave the app a real 2026: garlic, leeks and kale recorded as in, and two beds free. My comparisons are against my records as the panel has them.

I'm Callum, 52, near Aviemore (PH22 1AA), 250 m up in a frost hollow by the Spey. Four 3 × 1.2 m beds, a 6 × 18 ft polytunnel, rhubarb, raspberries and apples. Screenshots are in `scratchpad/testers/callum/`, and the two spreadsheet downloads are there too.

## Would it replace my spreadsheet?
Not yet, but it's a start. Settings → Spreadsheet of plantings gave `strathspey-plantings-2026-10-30.csv`:

`Bed or pot, Plant, How many, Laid out as, Stage, To sow by, Sown or planted, Up, Hardening off, Planted out, Growing, Flowering, Harvesting, Cleared, Times picked, Notes`

My dates came out correctly, one row a planting. I could paste that next to mine. **What's missing:**
- a variety;
- the first pick's date and the last, not just "Times picked";
- last year's rows: it's only what's in the garden now, so it doesn't build up;
- any way to bring my fifteen years in, or see "last year I sowed leeks on 5 April".

## The rotation
**"Telling it what grew changed nothing": reproduced, with the cause.** In Today → Next year → Draft it, choosing Potato under "What grew here in 2026?" changes nothing at all. A new button, "Add it to 2026", appears, and only that makes a change. At 1366 × 768 the button is below the bottom of the screen (y 1026), so you've no sign that anything's waiting. I chose from the drop-down and saw nothing. When I pressed the button, the draft moved: Potato (10) went to the next bed, and the garlic moved on.

**Mixed beds: not fixed.** My Roots bed holds leeks and kale, and they move as one: "Leek (20) Kale (7)", as "Onions and cabbage family". They go into the Garlic bed: "Onions → Onions and cabbage family (the same again: there's no other bed for them)". The app knows it's onions after onions, and says so. I'd have split the kale off to follow something else. It's honest, at least.

## What I like (keep it)
- **Own frost dates now move everything.** Broad beans went from 1 Feb to 10 Apr, and potatoes from 1 Apr to 22 May. That was my complaint.
- **Height and a frost hollow** in Settings. The first frost at 9 September is right.
- **Kale** stands until about 27 February, and it's "Probably ready to pick now". Last time it was finished on 30 October.
- **No more "Plant: Garlic"** after it's in. Today follows what I've recorded.
- **Feet work:** 6 ft × 18 ft gave a 5.49 × 1.83 m tunnel.
- **The photo's gone from the planting bar.** I checked every control at this size with the tray open, and none was covered.
- **The plan has room:** 958 × 570, or 958 × 354 with the tray open, up from about 935 × 300.
- **Today in spring reads right:** 15 April has leeks and broad beans, 25 May has potatoes and the tomato into the tunnel.
- **Backup** kept my frost dates and height in a fresh browser.

## What I don't like
Worst first.
1. **Potatoes harvested after the first frost.** "Harvesting about 20 Sep" with a first frost of 5 September. Second earlies are out by mid-August here.
2. **Leek plant-out is a month early, and the first harvest two months late.** Planted about 25 May, first harvest about 2 Jan. Those are my two leek dates and neither is right.
3. **Rotation: the button you can't see.** See above.
4. **Leeks and kale moved as a block.** Kale's a brassica.
5. **The hollow's last frost is two weeks too late.** The app's own default is nearer than the fuller answer.
6. **"Next: plant May" sits against the timeline.** With the estimate, the card said plant May while the timeline said 7 June. The two should come from one place.
7. **Tomatoes sown 1 March.** Four weeks early, and "earliest for a greenhouse" doesn't help a windowsill.
8. **Winter purslane in the tunnel:** "Ready about 23 Dec", then a Clear and compost job on 25 May with the crop's months running to April. That's fine, but the first date's well out.
9. **Apple "one year in three"** still isn't anywhere. Fine for a planner, but it says Ready to pick each year.

## Contradictions
- Potato: "Harvesting about 20 Sep" and "First frost about 5 Sep".
- Potato: "Next: plant May" and "Planted about 7 Jun 2027" with the estimate.
- Leek: "Harvest Sep–Mar" and "Ready to harvest from about 2 Jan".
- Rotation: the draft says "Nothing was recorded in the beds in 2026" when nothing's been sown yet, and "Nothing new goes in until it's out" for planned crops.

## What's superfluous
- "Your season, wrapped", and recipes on the leek page.
- Show beside each job.

## What's missing
- **Harvest dates tied to my season**, the way sowing dates now are.
- **Year on year:** last year's dates kept, and fed into next year.
- **Import** of my past dates.
- **Splitting a mixed bed** in the rotation.
- **A polytunnel** as a kind. Not essential.

## My top five changes
1. **Tie harvest dates to the frost dates,** as sowing now is. Nothing's harvested after the first frost unless it stands.
2. **Make "What grew here" act when it's chosen,** or show the button where it can be seen.
3. **Split mixed beds in the rotation,** by family.
4. **Check the frost hollow's spring figure** against a real cold site.
5. **Keep each year's dates,** so next year starts from them.
