# Feedback: Priya (12 Oct 2026, release 36)
I'm Priya, 34, in Bristol (BS6 5AA): two raised beds (2.4 × 1.2 m) in a small terraced garden, and about a dozen pots on the patio. I tested on a phone-sized screen (390 × 844) in short bursts, as I'd use it on the bus. I set up from scratch in list mode with the "Bulbs and autumn veg" kit, added my second bed and 12 pots, and put in garlic, autumn broad beans, corn salad, bulbs in pots, rosemary, thyme, parsley, a bay and a dwarf apple. I ticked jobs, opened a "how to", added a seed packet and sowed from it in the Shed, then checked the shopping list and next year's draft. I switched to the plan and drew a bed, and tried the share picture, 3D, Wrapped, search, help and harvest, then reloaded. About 75 minutes in all, which is far more than I'd ever give it in one go.

## Would I keep using it?
Probably, for now. On a Monday the Today list really is what I'd want: garlic, onion sets and bulbs, each with depth and feed, and a "How to" that explains words like "hardy" with one tap. The seed tin counting down when I sow is the kind of thing I'd pay for. What would make me drop it is the same thing as last time, in a new form. I sowed corn salad from my seed tin, and Today still said "Sow corn salad under cover" *and* "Plant out corn salad", both unticked. Broad beans show as "sow under cover" and "sow outside" at once, and ticking one leaves the other hanging. My bay and apple sit in "Running behind" for ever, and "Ready to pick: rosemary" comes back every month. If ticking still leaves junk behind by early November, I'll be back in Notion: a list I can't clear is worse than no list. And the Garden list not scrolling on my phone would end it on day one if it's real.

## What I like (keep it)
- **Setup** (welcome, then four steps): Enter on the postcode works now, "Bulbs and autumn veg" was pre-picked and right for October, and "How many?" when adding a pot let me add four bulb pots at once, auto-numbered "Bulb pot 1–4".
- **Today, top of the screen**: the photo is a slim band, and the jobs start straight under "Now" with tick boxes. "0 of 16 done this month" is a nice nudge. Nothing said "tomorrow".
- **Job text**: "About 2.5 cm deep. Plant single cloves, pointed end up… blood, fish and bone, a good handful a square metre." I'd act on that standing in the garden.
- **Glossary**: dotted words ("under cover", "hardy", "drill") open a short, plain definition with "Got it". This is excellent; please keep it.
- **How to** (from a job): "What you need" and "How", a time estimate ("About 15 minutes a row"), and a Done button that ticks the job.
- **Shopping list** (Today, Planning ahead, Open): the right words now, "Crocus bulbs, 16 bulbs", "Onion sets, overwintering, about 48", grouped into bulbs, compost and feed, with which pot each is for. Planted garlic dropped off it.
- **Seed tin** (Shed, Seed tin): adding a packet is quick, and "Sow some" took it from 200 to 176 seeds. The Shed tab name and its three tabs make sense now.
- **"Already in the ground"** now asks "It went in" (this month… over a year ago).
- **Next year** (Today, Draft it): "Taken until about June by broad bean, autumn-sown, already in for 2027" is exactly right, and "What grew here in 2026?" is a good idea.
- **No ⚠ on my tomato pot**: one cherry tomato in a 40 cm pot says "Nearly full". Fixed.
- **Toasts** are wide and readable, with Undo. Deleting a bed is instant, with Undo, not a scary dialog.
- **Help** (the ? on Today): four short numbered steps, readable.
- **Harvest** (planting, What's happened?): "First pick" and "A basket" took two taps. Lovely.

## What I don't like
Worst first.
1. **The Garden list doesn't scroll** (Garden tab, list mode). The page's main panel has scrolling switched off on Garden only; Today, Shed and Plants scroll fine. With 14 beds and pots, I couldn't reach anything below the first bed or two except by tapping buttons that jump the page. On a phone that means I can't see my pots at all. (See bug 1.)
2. **Sowing in the Shed doesn't touch the bed's planting or Today.** I sowed corn salad in a module tray from the seed tin. The tray appeared and the tin went down, but Today still says "Sow under cover: corn salad" and "Plant out: corn salad", and the Back bed card still says "Planned". The Shed's tray and the bed's row are two separate things that never meet. This was my number one complaint last time.
3. **Two ways to do one thing, both listed as jobs.** Autumn broad beans in the Back bed get "Sow under cover" *and* "Sow outside". I sowed outside and ticked it; the under-cover job stayed, and the bed card still shows a "Sow under cover" tag. It should be one job with "or".
4. **Jobs out of order.** Corn salad: "Plant out… now" sits above "Later this month: sow under cover". You can't plant out what isn't sown.
5. **Established plants treated as new.** My bay and apple, both "over a year ago", go straight into "Running behind: expected to be growing away by 22 Oct". It's behind, and yet the date is in the future. The apple also says "In flower from about 13 Oct", "Coming into flower" this week, and "Water in well… until the roots have found their way". After I logged a pick, it told me "Pick little and often: the more you pick, the more there is", which isn't how apples work. This is the stuff that makes me doubt the rest.
6. **Bulbs in pots are counted as if in the ground.** "Fill the bed" on a 35 cm pot gave 4 daffodils and called it "Nearly full"; moving 16 tulips into a pot silently cut them to 4. A 35 cm pot takes 10–15 daffodils, packed shoulder to shoulder. Twelve tulips vanished without a word, and there's no way to set the number afterwards (Change the details only has the date).
7. **Pot nags that never go away.** "Rosemary wants a pot about 45 cm across… Pot it on when you can" on a 25 cm pot, and the same for my bay in a 40 cm pot. Both are fine for years. It's the ⚠ problem again, just moved to herbs, and I can't dismiss it. It also reads "this herb pot 1 is 25 cm across", with my pot's name lower-cased mid-sentence.
8. **Switching to a plan makes a mess and then blames me.** The beds and pots "set out neatly" landed on top of each other: the bay over Raised bed 1, pots on the Back bed, the onions not visible. Then "8 things to check", six of them spacing clashes the app itself created, plus "tulip gets 5.5 h of sun in June" (tulips are over by June).
9. **The kit gave me a border I don't have**, and the broad beans went on a "Want to grow" list rather than in my second bed (the kit says "broad beans in the other", but the Garden preset only had one bed). When I deleted the border, its alliums stayed on as a job ("Ornamental allium on your Want to grow list") and on the shopping list with no count.
10. **Today is still long.** Below the jobs: To look out for, Running behind, Planning ahead, Getting started, In the kitchen, Room to grow, Wrapped, Garden journal, Install, Your garden. That's ten cards. On a bus I scroll past all of them.

## What's superfluous
- **3D** (Garden, Show: in 3D): a grey house wall fills the view and my beds are hidden behind it. "From above" didn't change it. For a 5 × 9 m terrace it's pointless; hide it unless someone asks.
- **Wrapped**: three plain green cards ("6 plantings, of 4 crops", which is wrong, as I have 15; "Busiest month October"; "Next year, try Courgette"). No pictures of my plants. Nobody would post this. Cut it until it can show real photos or plants.
- **"In the kitchen this week"** on Today: grape focaccia because I have a rosemary pot. Cute once; on Today every week it's clutter. Put it on the plant page.
- **"Ready to pick" as monthly jobs for herbs and bay.** Parsley, rosemary, thyme and bay are always there. They shouldn't be tick-box jobs every month.
- **"Weed the beds (Raised bed 1, Back bed, Bulb pot 1 and 8 more)"**: weeding pots isn't a job.
- **"Room to grow · Ideas for the lawn"**: I have no lawn that I can see; the preset made one I never chose.
- **Garden size in setup when I chose a list** ("Width 10, Depth 14"): it doesn't matter in a list, so ask only for a plan.
- **The colour bar legend** at the top of the Garden list (seed and seedling, growing, in flower, to pick, paler: expected): five keys to decode a thin bar I never looked at.
- **The welcome-back screen** on the first visit of the day ("The soil remembers how it's been treated"), with five buttons before Today. Just open Today.

## What's missing
- **One sowing, one record**: sowing from the tin should ask "Which bed is this for?" and link to that row, closing its sow job and dating its plant-out.
- **A way to change how many plants** after adding (Change the details only has the date).
- **Dense planting for bulbs in pots**, and "lasagne" layering.
- **"Established" for perennials**, so a year-old bay or apple skips first-year care and "running behind".
- **Dismiss or snooze for a check** ("I know, it's fine").
- **A follow-on crop in the next-year draft.** Both my beds are "taken until June / August", so the draft is "Use this for 2027 (0 plantings)". Tell me what can go in after the broad beans and the garlic.
- **A share picture I'd post**: still the scale plan, half of it grey house, with labels too small to read. I wanted an illustrated "my beds this month" card, with my plant names and the season's colours.
- **"Things to check" in list mode should take me to the things**, not the top of Garden.

## My top five changes
1. **Make the Garden list scroll**: set the Garden screen's scroll container to `overflow-y: auto` in list mode, as on Today, Shed and Plants.
2. **Link a Shed sowing to its bed row**: when sowing from the tin or Trays, ask "For which bed?" (pre-picking the planned row of that plant). That sets the row's sowing date, ticks its sow job, and dates its plant-out from the tray. Also, ticking any one sowing method closes the others for that planting.
3. **Add "Established" for perennials and trees**: anything added as "over a year ago" skips first-year care, "running behind" and flowering-from-planting dates; it uses the plant's normal calendar (an apple flowers in April) and never gives "pick little and often" to tree fruit.
4. **Bulb and pot counts that match real practice**: pots use bulb planting density (about 10–15 daffodils in 35 cm). Moving a planting into a smaller space asks "Keep 16 or fit 4?". "Change the details" lets me edit the number.
5. **Quiet the noise on Today and in checks**: one job per planting with "or" for alternatives; no monthly "ready to pick" or "weed" jobs for evergreen herbs and pots; a "Dismiss" on pot-size and spacing checks; and move recipes, Wrapped and "Room to grow" off Today into Garden or the plant pages.
