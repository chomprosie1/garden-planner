## Would I keep using it?
I'd keep it on the iPad this winter for Today's job list, the seed tin and the garden words, which are the best I've seen in an app. But I'm not giving up the notebook. Within twenty minutes of putting in what I actually have, the app told me:
- my sweet peas, sown last week, would flower on 9 November (then 10 March, once I corrected it);
- my two-year-old raspberries are "running behind" and coming into flower this week;
- my parsnips want picking in the morning with scissors.

The trouble isn't the plant pages, which are mostly right. It's what happens to a real planting once it's in the ground. That's exactly the part a notebook does well, and the part I'd want an app for. Until "already in the ground" and the fruit cage work, I'd be checking everything twice, and that's more work than the notebook, not less.

## What I like (keep it)
- **Extra large text** (Settings › Look and text size). Once found, it works on Today, the beds and the plant pages. The words are big and dark enough for me without glasses on.
- **Today's layout.** "Now" first, ticks beside each job, "Later this month" below, and no more sideways tiles or "tomorrow" on everything. The photo is a strip now, not half the screen.
- **The garden words** (dotted underlines and Help › Garden words). Clear, British and right: chitting with egg boxes, leaf mould "a year or two", "Sets… push them in with the tip just showing". I'd give the list to a beginner.
- **"How to" for garlic and onions** (Today › How to). "Garlic bulbs from a garden centre, not the supermarket" and "keeping the papery skins on" is what I'd tell my granddaughter.
- **The shopping list words are fixed.** "Garlic, about 24 cloves (2 bulbs or so)" and "Onion sets, about 40", under "Bulbs, sets and seed potatoes". It took off what's in my seed tin ("Your tin has about 40 seeds").
- **"It went in" month** when ticking "Already in the ground". My leeks show "Planted out · Harvest Dec–Apr", which is right.
- **Late leek, parsnip, garlic and raspberry plant pages.** The spacings, depths, feeding ("nothing after May" for garlic), pests (allium leaf miner "in spring and autumn") and storage are sound.
- **The seed tin** (Shed › Seed tin) with variety name, count and sow-by. That's my seed box in an app.
- **The journal** can be for one bed or the whole garden, with a date.
- **The postcode search** took Enter and found Shropshire first time.
- **The toasts** are wide enough to read now.

## What I don't like
Worst first.

1. **Fruit is treated like a crop that started this week.** Fruit cage: a summer raspberry planted "over a year ago" shows "In flower from about 13 Oct". Today says "Running behind… expected to be growing away by 22 Oct", and "To look out for this week" says "Coming into flower" and "Ready to pick" for both raspberry and blackcurrant. In October they're going dormant. If an app tells me raspberries are flowering in October, I stop believing its dates for everything else.
2. **"Already in the ground" gets the stage wrong for anything that isn't a transplant.** Sweet peas sown this month in the cold frame became "Planted out 12 Oct", "Water in well to settle the soil round the roots", and "In flower from about 9 Nov". Parsnips sown in April are "Planted out", although nobody plants out a parsnip. Leeks in since June are "Running behind… by 6 Jul". It needs to ask me sown, growing or ready, not just the month.
3. **Correcting the sweet peas made a different mistake.** I set the stage to Sown (Correct a mistake). Then the card said "Next: plant out Apr–May", the timeline said "Planted out about 23 Nov" and "Flowering about 10 Mar", and November's list says "Probably flowering by now… pick flowers every few days". Three answers, all on one planting. And only one of the two rows changed: the other still said "Planted out".
4. **Next year's draft is no use to me** (Today › Next year › Draft it). Bed 4 "takes the crops from Fruit cage". Bed 2 and Bed 4 are "free for whatever you like", though my broad beans and garlic will stand in them till June and July. Every bed says "No crops move here". I do my rotation on the back page of the notebook in five minutes. This would take me longer to unpick.

In the Shed's Sow seeds form, "Where" offers only "Windowsill (set up for you)", and it chose a module tray for sweet peas when the advice right above says root trainers in a cold frame. Help says I can "Raise seedlings in here" and set the greenhouse's warmth, but that isn't in the list-mode bed settings.
6. **The fruit cage holds almost nothing.** A 4 × 3 m cage was "about half full" with one raspberry and two blackcurrants. A third blackcurrant and two gooseberries got "no room left". Raspberries only offer "One" or "A number", not a row, and raspberries are always grown in rows. Mine has a 3 m row of canes, three blackcurrants and two gooseberries.
7. **Wrong verbs.** Garlic gets "Mark as sown", "Sown" once ticked, "Sow outside" on its calendar and "Sow in batches". Parsnips are "Ready to pick" and "Planted out". "Sow in the shed" sounds like a dark shed. These are small, but they're the words that tell me whether a gardener wrote it.
8. **The sizes I typed got lost.** I typed 3 m for Bed 2, then changed "How sunny is it?" and pressed Done. The bed stayed 2.40 m. That happened twice. Also, "Another like this" quietly renamed my original bed ("Bed 2 (legumes)" became "Bed 2 (legumes) 1").
9. **Counts don't agree.** I asked for 24 garlic: the bed says "Garlic × 24 · 2 rows", but the planting sheet says "Plants 20". I chose "Traditional rows" in Settings, yet "Fill the bed" put 90 broad beans in a 20 cm block. In rows that would be about 30 to 50, and the shopping list then wants seed "for about 90 plants".
10. **The same job twice, and one place doesn't update another.** One planting of broad beans is both "Sow under cover" and "Sow outside" in Now. Winter purslane is "Plant out" now and "Sow under cover" later this month. I sowed 32 broad beans in root trainers in the Shed, and Today's "Sow under cover: broad bean" stayed unticked. "Sow sweet pea" stayed after I'd marked them sown.
11. **Text size is hard to find.** It's the eighth section down in Settings, below backups and gardens, and after tapping Extra large the page jumped away to Reminders. The reminder checkboxes are tiny and sit centred above their labels, apart from them, at every size. The drop-downs in Next year don't grow.
12. **The plan on an iPad at Extra large.** The garden is drawn in a strip about 400 px wide, beside a panel of nine warnings. They say my rows are "225 mm apart; they need about 300 mm", but the app laid those rows out itself. Labels overlap ("Bed 3 (Bed 4 (potatoes…"). The month letters under the slider still run together. Opening "Beds and pots" pushed the plan off the top and left half the screen blank.

## My top five changes
1. **Give fruit and other perennials a yearly round from the calendar, not from the planting date.** Dormant from November to February, flowering and fruiting in their months, with pruning and feeding jobs. They're never "running behind" and never "ready to pick" out of season. Add a fruit cage as a kind of bed, raspberries in rows, and bushes at their real spacing.
2. **Make "Already in the ground" ask what stage it's at** (sown, up, growing, ready to harvest), not just the month. Then time autumn sowings by the season: October sweet peas and broad beans flower and crop in late May–June. Take every date (card, timeline, Today, next month) from one source, so they can't disagree. When I correct one row, change all the rows of that sowing.
3. **Rebuild the next-year draft round the real beds.** Only veg beds rotate. Fruit, the border and the greenhouse stay put. Crops planted this autumn count as next year's crop in their bed, and crops standing now aren't "ran behind".
4. **Let the cold frame and greenhouse be places to raise plants in list mode.** Offer them under "Where" in Sow seeds, and default sweet peas and broad beans to root trainers in the frame. Don't count pots in a frame at final spacing. Sowing in the Shed should tick the matching Today job and the planting.
5. **Use the right word and the right "How to" for each kind of crop**: plant garlic, sets and fruit; sow parsnips; lift roots with a fork; pick fruit and leaves. Also make varieties searchable and recordable on a planting ('Aquadulce Claudia', 'Radar', 'Musselburgh'), with the autumn kind named on the shopping list.
