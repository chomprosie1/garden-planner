- [Priya](feedback-2026-10-29-priya.md)
- [Margaret](feedback-2026-10-29-margaret.md)

- [Sam](feedback-2026-10-29-sam.md)
- [Ruth](feedback-2026-10-29-ruth.md)
- [Callum](feedback-2026-10-29-callum.md)

All six are personas played by Claude against the running app, at each person's screen size. Their scores are a consistent way to compare rounds and find problems. They are not market evidence. In particular, what they'd pay is a hint, not a price; only paying users can settle that.

How it was run:
- Each tester was told their feedback is valued and will be acted on.
- They were told it's the app being tested, not them, and that they could skip or stop any task and say why.
- All six said in "Before I started" that the brief was clear and they were comfortable.
- The new three didn't see anyone's earlier feedback, so their scores aren't anchored to the others'.
- Nobody was told the targets.

## Where the new three's feedback differs

What only the new testers found:
- **Ruth: accessibility.** The originals never had cause to find these:
  - Today's tick boxes are 22 × 22 px, half the 44 to 48 px minimum.
  - Plan buttons are 34 to 38 px and dropdowns 34 px tall.
  - Tapping a job's words opens its how-to instead of ticking it.
  - The plan's label tells a screen reader about "a list of features beside the plan" that doesn't exist.
  - Controls don't grow with Extra large text.
  - She couldn't check Android's own text size or real TalkBack; the screen reader was judged from what the page announces.
- **Ruth: the plan can move plants without asking.** One steady drag on a raised bed pulled its six strawberries out onto the lawn. It was undoable, but this is the plan putting plants in the wrong place.
- **Sam: the app's model of a garden leaks into a balcony.**
  - Watering ignores cover: "Nothing likely to need watering, 4 mm of rain", for pots under the balcony above. Watering also only shows on the plan.
  - Shade set on each container is dropped on the plan: Today raises 19 checks and the plan 6.
  - Setup never asks which way the balcony faces.
  - Greenhouse, cold-frame and leaf-mould jobs appear on a balcony.
  - The shade filter says 22 plants and shows 3.
  - A pot 25 m across is accepted.
- **Callum: hardy crops ignore the local season.**
  - Sowing and planting dates are 4 to 10 weeks early for Strathspey.
  - Typing his own frost dates moved only tender plant-outs, by about ten days. Potatoes still go out on 1 April and broad beans are sown on 1 February.
  - The first frost (5 Oct) is about a month late. The climate is blended from Inverness, Aberdeen and Edinburgh, with no correction for height or a frost hollow.

  Margaret's Shawbury dates are also still blended with Birmingham and Manchester.
- **Callum: the laptop is untested ground.** At 1366 × 768, the season photo covers "Already in the ground", "Change plant" and "Done" on the planting bar. Plants he said were in were saved as Planned, twice. The plan "jumps" on entering planting mode, so a leek row landed on the lawn. The plan is "a letterbox" on that screen.

## Where all six agree

- **Losing the garden.** Priya, Margaret, Jordan and Ruth each named "lost if the browser is cleared" as a reason to cancel. Callum wants an export he can open. Nobody would pay for something kept only in one browser.
- **Today must follow what you've recorded.**
  - Margaret: garlic, onions, broad beans and sweet peas recorded as in still had sow or plant jobs.
  - Callum: "plant garlic" after he marked it planted.
  - Priya: autumn under-cover sowings shown with dates before the sowing.
- **"Done" in a how-to ticks the job.** Ruth and Margaret both ticked jobs by closing a card; Margaret ticked six.
- **Jobs for things you don't have.** Jordan, Priya and Sam got greenhouse and cold-frame jobs with no greenhouse or cold frame.
- **One figure, everywhere.** Each tester found two screens disagreeing:
  - checks (19 against 6, and 2 against 1);
  - feed (bonemeal against blood, fish and bone);
  - room (strawberry tower "590%" next to 144%; the fruit cage "half full" refusing a third bush);
  - counts (×20 on the plan, 40 cloves on the list; 13 plantings for 8 rows);
  - ends of harvest (parsnips "finished by 10 Dec" beside "Oct–Mar"; kale "finished 30 Oct" beside "Sep–Mar").
- **Plan labels overlap** (Jordan, Margaret, Priya).

## Causes confirmed in the code

- **Jobs don't follow a planting recorded another way.** A job counts as done only if its key is in `jobsDone` (`src/ui/views/Beds.tsx:96`, `Plan.tsx:295`). A sowing recorded this month through "Already in the ground", "Mark as sown" or the Shed is still listed, so that a ticked job stays for the month (`src/calendar/jobs.ts:259`), but nothing ticks it. Fix: work out "done" from the planting as well as the tick. Size S to M; no schema change.
- **Hardy sowings ignore the local season.** Sow jobs use the plant's fixed `sowing.months` (`sowingKinds`, `src/calendar/jobs.ts:114`). The local shift exists (`seasonShift`, `src/lifecycle/growth.ts:158`), but only the projection uses it. Fix: shift sowing and planting months by the garden's season, and correct the climate for height. Size M to L; needs a named source for the lapse rate and frost hollows.
- **"Make your first bed" misses a bare garden.** `needsFirstBed` (`src/planting/firstBed.ts`) looks for a lawn surface. "Just the space" makes a boundary and a house with no lawn drawn, so the lawn-only garden it was built for never sees it. Fix: a boundary with nowhere to grow counts as grass. Also, a kit's beds are assumed to exist, so setup should ask whether they're there yet. Size S.

The rest are as reported, with steps and screenshots in each feedback file. Each needs reproducing before it's fixed.
