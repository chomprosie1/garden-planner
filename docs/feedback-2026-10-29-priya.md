# Feedback: Priya (29 Oct 2026, release 47)
**Before I started:** The brief was clear and I was comfortable with it. I made three choices of my own. I picked "Patio or yard" rather than "Garden", because last time "Garden" gave me a lawn and a border I don't have. I used the "Autumn planting" kit, which was pre-picked. I can't trace a loop with the test tool's straight-line drag, so for the hand-drawn bed I fed the plan a finger-path round a rectangle. Everything else I did by tapping, as I would on my phone.

I'm Priya, 34, in Bristol (BS6 5AA): two raised beds (2.4 × 1.2 m) in a small terraced garden and about a dozen pots on the patio. I tested on a 390 px phone screen in short bursts. I'd recommend it to a friend: 6/10. I'd say "brilliant for the monthly jobs; don't believe the dates on the bed cards yet; ignore the plan".

## Would I pay for it?
Maybe. I'd pay about £15 a year, or a one-off £20. I would not pay monthly: I garden in bursts, and a monthly fee would get cancelled in December. For comparison, I pay for a plant-ID app (about £25 a year) and I'd consider GrowVeg-type planners at £25–30. Before I paid, three things would have to be true: the dates on a planting were right; my garden was backed up somewhere other than this one browser (I change phones); and nothing contradicted itself on the same screen. I'd cancel if I caught it giving a wrong date twice in a month, or if I lost my garden to a cleared browser.

## Would I keep using it?
Probably, and for longer than last time. The main reason I nearly dropped it on 12 Oct is gone. I sowed winter lettuce from my seed tin, the Shed asked "For: Raised bed 2, where it's planned", Today's sow job ticked itself, the tin went from 300 to 276, and the bed changed to "Sown". Broad beans are one job with "or". Nothing is "running behind", and pots are no longer in the weeding job. That's a list I can clear on the bus. What would make me drop it is the bed card. The first thing it says about my lettuce is "Sown · Next: plant out from about 24 Sep", and the timeline puts hardening off and planting out in September, before the seeds went in. If that's still there when I next open the bed in mid-November, I'll stop trusting the dates. Then I'm back to a Notion page and the back of the seed packet.

## What I like (keep it)
- **Setup** (welcome, then four steps): the postcode with Enter, "Just a list" marked Simplest, and "Patio or yard" is a fair match. The kit was right for late October.
- **One job per crop** (Today): "Sow outside, or under cover in a cold frame: Broad bean". The How to has two tabs for the two ways, and "Done: sow outside" ticks it and the header changes to "Sow outside".
- **The Shed links to the bed** (Shed, Seed tin, Sow some): "For: Raised bed 2, where it's planned", the tin counts down, and Today and the bed follow. Ticking a sow job on Today also puts the tray in the Shed under "Sown for the plan". This was my number-one complaint, twice.
- **The broad bean How to**: "Make a hole for each seed with a dibber… Drop one seed in each hole… Spacing: 15 cm apart each way… Mice and birds like big seeds: a net or a few holly leaves." That's right now, and it's what I'd want standing in the garden.
- **Moves ask first** (planting ···, Move to another bed): "Thyme pot has room for 4 of the 24 tulip plants… Move all 24, closer together / Move 4, leave the rest". Moving the rest back merged them into "Tulip × 24".
- **You can edit the count** (planting, Change the details, Plants in this row).
- **Bulbs in pots at real density**: Fill gave 34 crocus, 16 tulips and 34 grape hyacinths in a 35 cm pot. Finally.
- **"I know, it's fine"** on checks, with "Fine: that check won't be raised again." Thank you.
- **Already in the ground** asks the month and "It's now: Planted / Growing / Flowering / Harvesting", and picks Growing for "over a year ago". No more "running behind" for my bay and apple.
- **Next year's draft** leaves pots, the bay, the apple and the herbs alone, and says what follows: "Free again from about September: follow on with spinach, winter purslane or corn salad."
- **The Garden list scrolls**, all twelve beds and pots.
- **Pots on the shopping list**: "Tulip bulbs, 40 bulbs, for Pot 1 and Bulb pot 2", plus "Multi-purpose compost, about 165 litres". Planted garlic and sown beans dropped off it.
- **Today is shorter.** Recipes, Wrapped and Room to grow have gone, and done jobs are crossed out, not deleted.
- **Drawing by hand** (plan, Advanced tools, Draw, Bed): I went round the shape with one finger and got a clean rectangle, with "Bed added. Tap it to name it" and Undo. Dragging a bed to move it works too.

## What I don't like
Worst first.
1. **Autumn sowings under cover get a timeline in the past.** After I sowed winter lettuce (from the Shed) and corn salad (by ticking on Today), both bed cards say "Sown 29 Oct · Next: plant out from about 24 Sep" and "Ready to harvest from about 5 Nov". The timeline reads: Up about 6 Nov, Hardening off about 17 Sep, Planted out about 24 Sep, Growing about 15 Oct. November's jobs then list "Ready to pick: Lettuce, winter" for seeds I sowed today. It's the first line on the bed, and it's nonsense. It matters because it's exactly the kind of wrong date that makes me doubt the jobs that are right. (Bugs 1, 2.)
2. **The plan is still a pile-up.** After switching to a plan, the patio from the preset is about 2.5 m square, Raised bed 2 and nine pots sit in a line outside the garden, and the bay and apple are drawn as overlapping 3 m trees. Then come "4 things to check": "Bay and apple are 1.23 m apart; they need about 2.25 m" (they're in separate pots), "Apple and rosemary…", and "The block of tulip and the block of tulip overlap" (made by the app's own merge). The toast when I added my second bed in list mode had already said "there wasn't room inside your garden's outline, so it's set just outside it". I never drew an outline. (Bugs 6, 7.)
3. **The kit contradicts itself.** "Autumn planting" put garlic and broad beans in the same 1.2 m bed, then flagged in red: "Broad bean, autumn-sown and garlic are in Bed. They're usually kept apart." Its first move made a warning. (Bug 3.)
4. **"Room" means three different things.** Raised bed 1 says "Plenty of room", yet adding a row of corn salad says "There's no room left in Raised bed 1 for another row". Bulb pot 3 says "Nearly full" with 34 grape hyacinths, yet moving tulips says "Bulb pot 3 has room for 16". Once I'd moved them it was "Over-full… 175%", with two warnings saying the same thing. Raised bed 2 is "Plenty of room" while its bean row warns "46 mm apart; they need about 150 mm". I can't tell which number to believe. (Bugs 4, 5.)
5. **List mode gives me one 1.05 m row for broad beans in a 2.4 m bed.** I raised the count to 16 and was told "70 mm apart… Fewer plants, or a longer row, would fit", but there's no way to make the row longer or add a second one in list mode. I'd grow a double row of about 20 here. I had to tap "I know, it's fine" on a correct planting.
6. **Wrong fruit advice for the apple.** The apple's How to is soft-fruit text: "Pick in the dry, every few days… soft fruit keeps a day or two." Apples are lifted and twisted, and keep for months. Today also says "Ready to pick: Apple" while the planting says "Growing, next: flowering about 23 Mar" (late March is early for Bristol; April is usual). (Bug 8.)
7. **The bay's timeline loops.** "Harvesting about 19 Nov, Growing about 1 Jan, Harvesting about 22 Jan, Growing about 1 Jan", and "Ready to harvest from about 19 Nov" for an evergreen marked "Harvest All year". (Bug 9.)
8. **Pot nags are still on by default.** "Bay wants a pot about 45 cm… this bay pot is 40 cm… Pot it on" (and the same for rosemary at 25 cm). I can dismiss them now, which helps. But "Pot on" offers to move the bay into my Apple pot or my tulip pot, both already full. And it's still "this bay pot is", lower-cased mid-sentence.
9. **Bulb feed disagrees with itself.** Every bulb job says "bonemeal, a small handful in the hole"; the shopping list buys "Blood, fish and bone, for… tulip, daffodil", with no bonemeal on it.
10. **The welcome-back screen is still there** on every reload, with five buttons ("Enter my garden", "See my tasks", "About my garden", "What's new", "Continue to Today"), three of which go roughly to Today. I asked last time for it to just open Today.

## What's superfluous
- **The welcome-back screen.** Open Today. If you must, put the quote as one line at the top of Today.
- **Generic November jobs I can't do**: "Open the greenhouse vents a little on mild mornings" and "Keep the cold frame lid open by day" when I have neither, and "Plant tulip bulbs" in Around the garden when my two tulip pots already have their own jobs.
- **"Ready to pick" for thyme and parsley** every month (rosemary and bay have rightly gone). Thyme's tip in late October is "Trim after flowering". Herbs are always there; they're not tick-box jobs.
- **"Your garden" card on Today**, with a plan thumbnail in list mode showing my pots strung outside the patio. It looks broken. In a list, show the counts and the check link, not a picture.
- **"Getting started 3 of 4 done"**: the fourth item is "Download a backup". Make backup its own one-line nudge and drop the checklist.
- **"Tulip × 24 · 2 blocks"**: I don't need to know about blocks in a pot.
- **Soil chips with no heading** in the bed edit ("The garden's / Clay / Loam…"); I had to guess they were soil.
- **"Fill the bed" as the default for a second crop** in a bed that already has one. It nearly crammed lettuce in on top of my beans. Default to "A row" when the bed isn't empty.

## What's missing
- **Lengthen or add rows in list mode**: a "Rows: 1 / 2" or a row-length field beside the count.
- **Backup off the device**: Google Drive, iCloud or email, so the app's first promise isn't "kept only in this browser".
- **A quiet list of done jobs**: ticked jobs stay at the top of Today, in prime space. Sink them under a "Done (3)" line.
- **Follow-on crops I can tap**: the next-year draft names "kale, purple sprouting broccoli or cabbage" but doesn't let me add one.
- **Lasagne planting**: tulips under grape hyacinths in one pot is the classic way, and the app calls it over-full.
- **Search for "salad"** finding lettuce, rocket and mizuna, and a dwarf apple (rootstock) as a choice. "dwarf apple" finds nothing.
- **Search in list mode**: "what to sow now" finds nothing in Plants, and there's no search-everything button outside the plan.
- **A frost date I believe**: "First frost about 3 Nov" feels early for inner Bristol; mid-to-late November is usual here.

## My top five changes
1. **Fix the projection for autumn sowings under cover**: start every stage date from the actual sowing date, so no projected date is ever earlier than the sowing. Winter lettuce sown 29 Oct should read about "up 6 Nov, plant out late Nov to early Dec, pick from February". No "Ready to pick" job until it has been planted out. Add a test that no projected stage date comes before the sow date.
2. **Use one "room" calculation everywhere**: the bed badge, the Add-a-row check, the move dialog ("room for N" minus what's already there) and the over-full warnings should all use the same number. Collapse "Too crowded" and "over-full" into one message. Allow bulb layering (lasagne) in pots without calling it over-full.
3. **On switching to a plan, make the garden fit the beds**: grow the boundary or patio to hold every bed and pot added in the list, set pots on the paving, draw established trees in pots at pot size (not a 3 m canopy), and skip spacing checks between plants in different pots.
4. **Fix the kit and the fruit**: the Autumn planting kit should make two beds (garlic and onions in one, broad beans in the other) or not warn about its own layout. Give the apple a tree-fruit How to ("lift and twist; store the sound ones cool"). Use a once-a-year timeline for evergreens like bay. Add dwarf apple as a kind.
5. **Trim Today and the start**: open straight to Today on return; sink done jobs into a collapsed "Done (n)"; drop greenhouse and cold-frame jobs when there's neither; drop "Ready to pick" for thyme and parsley; replace the plan thumbnail in list mode with a line of counts; and let me set rows and row length in list mode.
