# Feedback: Ruth (29 Oct 2026, release 47)
**Before I started:** The brief was clear and I was comfortable with it. I had to make a few assumptions. I chose "Garden" as my space, then reshaped it, because nothing offered "three raised beds and some pots". I took "set the text as large as you need" to mean the app's own setting, since I can't test whether it follows my phone's system text size. TalkBack was judged from what a screen reader would announce, not from a real phone.

I'm Ruth, 67, a retired teacher in Norwich (NR4 7AA). I have rheumatoid arthritis in my hands and dry macular degeneration in my left eye. I used an Android-sized screen (412 × 915) with the app's text at Extra large, because that's how I'd set it. I set up three 1.8 × 0.9 m raised beds and four pots by the back door in list mode. Then I put in garlic to plant, tulips, daffodils and crocuses in the pots, 12 established strawberries, and thyme, chives, rosemary and parsley. I worked through Today, ticked a job, read the "how to"s, the plant pages and Settings, and tried the plan view. It took about an hour and a half at my pace. I'd recommend it to a friend: 6/10. I'd say "use the list, not the plan".

## Would I pay for it?
**Maybe.** I'd pay £10 to £15 as a one-off, or about £10 a year, but not a monthly charge. I pay for Gardeners' World magazine now, roughly £50 a year, and that's the comparison: it tells me what to do this month, with big pictures.

Before I paid, it would have to:
- give jobs for what's already growing, not just what's being planted;
- make the tick boxes big enough to hit first time;
- stop contradicting itself about feeds.

I'd cancel if I lost my garden by clearing the browser, which the app warns could happen, or if the jobs stayed generic all winter.

## Would I keep using it?
Probably, for the Today list. Seeing "Garlic in Raised bed 1, about 2.5 cm deep, a handful of blood, fish and bone a square metre" with a "How to" underneath is exactly what I want on a phone I hold close to my face. But I came with three autumn jobs and it only knew about one of them: garlic, yes. Bulbs, partly: crocus and daffodil yes, but tulips are "next month". Strawberries to tidy: nothing at all. For an experienced gardener, the value is in reminders for the things already in the ground, and that's the weakest part. I'd give it the winter and see whether it says anything useful about my strawberries and herbs by March.

## What I like (keep it)
- **The choice of a list in setup** (Step 2, "Just a list of my beds · Simplest"). I set up all my beds and pots without drawing anything. That's the most important thing in the app for me.
- **"Another like this"** in a bed's ··· menu. It copies the bed and numbers it (Raised bed 2, 3). That took three taps instead of filling in the form three times.
- **"How many?" when adding pots.** I typed 4 and got Back door pot 1 to 4 in one go.
- **The postcode worked first time,** and it says plainly what's sent where.
- **The "How to" sheets** (Today → How to). What you need, numbered steps, and roughly how long it takes. The garlic one ("from a garden centre, not the supermarket", "tip about 2.5 cm below the surface", "garlic hates competing") is right.
- **Dotted words** such as "bonemeal" and "hand fork" that explain themselves.
- **Undo after every delete or add,** without a scary "Are you sure?".
- **Screen-reader names on the controls.** Each tick box says "Done: plant, Garlic in Raised bed 1 (2 rows)". The ··· buttons say "More for Raised bed 1". The year chart reads "Plant: February–March, October–December". Someone has thought about this.
- **The shopping list** (Today → Planning ahead). It shows bulbs by number and compost in litres for the pots.
- **The strawberry page says "replace plants every three years" and "cut off the old leaves after fruiting".** That's correct.
- **Ticked jobs stay on the list, struck through.** I can see what I've done.

## What I don't like
1. **The plan view moved my plants when I meant to move a bed** (Garden in plan mode). One drag on Raised bed 2 pulled a row of six strawberries out of the bed and onto the lawn. My drag was perfectly steady, and my real hands would do worse. I only noticed because the warning count went up. Undo fixed it, but I'd never trust the plan after that.
2. **No jobs for established plants.** My 12 strawberries "already in the ground" got no autumn job: no tidying, no runners, no old leaves. The herbs got "Ready to pick", including chives at the end of October, when they're dying back. Nothing says to leave the rosemary alone over winter. The app only seems to think about planting.
3. **Tick boxes are tiny** (22 × 22 px on Today). Tapping the words of a job opens its How to instead of ticking it. With stiff fingers, I either miss the box or open something I didn't want.
4. **"Done" at the bottom of a How to ticks the job.** I pressed it to mean "I've finished reading", and my weeding was marked done.
5. **It doesn't know my beds are waist-high or that I work sitting down.** Weeding says "run the hoe just under the surface", and I can't hoe a bed at chest height. A bed's "Height" field exists, hidden in the plan's details, but nothing uses it. Raised beds dry out faster and need topping up with compost, and none of that is mentioned.
6. **The text size is hard to find.** It isn't on the welcome screen or in setup. I found it at the top of Settings, behind an unlabelled "sliders" icon in the corner of a photo. Extra large is only about a third bigger than Standard, and the bottom menu labels stay at about 15 px.
7. **Feeds contradict each other.** Today and the How to say "bonemeal, a small handful in the hole" for crocus and daffodil. The shopping list says to buy blood, fish and bone "for garlic, tulip, daffodil", and doesn't list bonemeal at all.
8. **Tulips aren't in "Sow or plant now"** on 29 October. I had to search for them, and then they showed as "Next: plant Nov–Dec". Late October is fine for tulips in Norfolk, and my bulbs are sitting in the shed.
9. **Pale and thin details.** The stage line on a planting's Timeline ("Flowering · Harvesting", with pale ring dots) and the thin coloured bar under each plant in the list are hard to see. The ··· buttons are three faint dots, and I wouldn't guess that's where "Name, size, sun and soil" lives.
10. **The undo message vanishes after about five seconds** and covers the bottom of the screen while it's there.

## Contradictions
- **Plant checks.** Today says "! 2 things to check in your planting", while the plan's chip says "1 thing to check" for the same garden at the same moment (45, 46).
- **Feeds for bulbs.** Today and the How to say bonemeal in the hole; the shopping list says blood, fish and bone for tulip and daffodil, and leaves bonemeal off the list (53).
- **Strawberry dates.** The plant page says "In flower: May" and "Harvest Jun–Jul". The planting says "In flower from about 23 Apr", and its Timeline says "Harvesting about 25 May". The Timeline's "Likely next" also lists "Flowering about 23 Apr" twice, with no years (39).
- **Spacing.** The Plants list says strawberry "40 cm apart" and garlic "15 cm apart". My plantings say 300 mm and 120 mm. It's bed versus row spacing, but the list doesn't say which.
- **Plantings count.** "13 plantings" for 8 things listed. Garlic × 30 in 2 rows counts as 2, strawberries in 2 rows as 2, and parsley × 3 as 3.
- **Getting started.** In list mode it says "3 of 4 done". After switching to the plan it says "4 of 6", and adds "Point the north arrow north".
- **Room in a bed.** Adding one rosemary to Raised bed 3 was accepted and made it "over-full: 118%". Asking for two said "There's no room left". Once (I couldn't repeat it) that message named Raised bed 2 when I was adding to Raised bed 3.

## What's superfluous
- **Recipes, storing and "In the kitchen"** on every plant page. I can find a jam recipe. It doubles the scrolling for someone who reads with her nose on the glass.
- **Looks (Cottage, Heritage, Allotment, Modern, Minimal), seasonal photos, "Photo month", "Soft shadows on the plan".** Six settings about decoration and none about contrast. I'd swap them all for one high-contrast option.
- **The big photo at the top of Today and Garden,** with its credit pill. It pushes my jobs down the screen.
- **The "Put it on your home screen" and "Garden journal" cards on Today,** every day. Ask once.
- **"Make my own version", "Inspire me", "Want to grow it?"** I'd never use them.
- **"About 17 h of direct sun a day in June"** on a bed, and tulip warnings about June sun. Tulips flower in April.
- **3D and the plan in general,** for me. Hide the plan entirely when someone picks the list, including the "Want to see it laid out?" nudge at the bottom of every Garden page.

## What's missing
- The **text size on the first screen**, and a **high-contrast look**.
- **Autumn and winter jobs for established plants**: strawberries (clear old leaves and runners, weed, mulch), chives (die back, cut down), rosemary and thyme (no hard pruning now), and bulbs in pots (feed after flowering, deadhead, let the leaves die back).
- **"Raised bed / I work sitting down"** on a bed, so the advice changes: hand tools instead of a hoe, watering more often, topping up compost, and lighter pots.
- **"Already in the ground" further back than "Over a year ago"**, which saved as 1 Oct 2025. My strawberries went in three years ago, and that matters because the app itself says to replace them every three years.
- A way to say "I've already got these" so bulbs I've bought come off the shopping list without ticking them one by one.
- **Crops-rotate switched off by default** for fruit and herbs. Every bed starts with "Its crops move round the beds each year" ticked, even though the note under it says to leave it off for fruit and herbs. Will "Next year, with the crops rotated" move my strawberries?
- **Read aloud** for a How to, for my tired-eye evenings.

## My top five changes
1. **Make jobs easy to tick.** Make every tick box and icon button at least 48 × 48 px, and let a tap anywhere on a job row tick it, with "How to" as its own button. In the How to, rename "Done" to "Mark as done" and add a plain "Close".
2. **Give established plants their own seasonal jobs.** Strawberries (October: clear old leaves and runners, weed, mulch), herbs (cut chives down, don't prune rosemary or thyme hard in autumn), and bulbs in pots after flowering. Add a "planted 2 or 3 or more years ago" choice, and show tulips under "plant now" from mid-October.
3. **Offer the text size and a new high-contrast look on the welcome screen and in setup step 1.** Make Extra large at least 150%, and scale the bottom menu, chips and plan labels with it.
4. **Add "This bed is raised (waist height) / I work sitting down" to a bed's settings.** It should change the advice: hand fork rather than hoe, water more often, top up compost each spring, and lighter pots.
5. **Make the plan safe or optional.** A drag that starts on a planting inside a bed should move the whole bed, or ask first. Add "Move" buttons (up, down, left, right) and position fields instead of drag-only. Give TalkBack the list of features the plan's label promises. Then make "things to check", the plantings count and the feed advice come from one place, so every screen agrees.
