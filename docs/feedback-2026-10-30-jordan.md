2. **Ticking still gives nothing back.** "1 of 10 done". Strava would have made something of my first bed.
3. **The count went up when I ticked.** "0 of 9 done" became "1 of 10".
4. **Getting started still says "Point the north arrow north".** I still don't know why I'd care, though it's now the only thing left on the list.
5. **The cost isn't mentioned.** 15 to 22 bags, plus 4 more, plus bulbs. For a renter trying it out, that's a real amount, and I'd want to see it before I start. Maybe also a smaller way in: one bed first.
6. **The compost weight reads oddly.** "About 150 litres (4 bags of 40 litres)… about 150 kg once it's wet" is fine, but nobody says that's four pots you won't be able to move.
7. **"Seed potatoes, about 11 a square metre".** I can't judge it, but the plant page says 30 cm each way for a bed and 35 cm with 65 cm rows, and I don't know which to buy for.
8. **A few words remain:** "slightly acidic" and "well-drained" on the potato page, and "Half-hardy" against "Tender". I didn't re-check whether they're explained now.
