# Jobs sweep, November 2026

Diagnostic only; nothing was fixed. 521 plants swept. Generated by tests/jobs-sweep.test.ts (run with SWEEP=1).

How plant jobs were told from general ones: a job counts as plant-specific when its `plantId` equals the swept plant (general jobs such as "Water the garden", "Weed the beds", cardboard and bed-making all have an empty `plantId`). Settings used: weeding and feeding on (the app default), today fixed at the 15th of each month, no weather. Established means `makePlanting(..., growing=true)`, which is stage "transplanted", with no dates, so it counts as settled. A weed is always created at that stage. "care" means a tidy job flagged `care`; "clear" means a tidy job flagged `clears`.
