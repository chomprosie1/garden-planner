## Would I pay for it?
**Maybe**, and not yet.
- **How much, and how:** about £12 to £15 a year, or a £10 to £15 one-off. Not monthly: gardening is seasonal, and I'd cancel a monthly plan in December.
- **What I pay for now:** Duolingo Super and Strava, each roughly £5 to £8 a month. Both earn it by getting me to come back every day. This app doesn't do that yet. Ticking a job off gives nothing back: no moment, no streak.
- **Before I'd pay:** it has to get me from lawn to beds (what to buy, how much, which weekend). I'd also want a month of it being right: jobs that match what's actually in my garden, and a shopping list I could take to a garden centre without second-guessing it.
- **What would make me cancel:**
  - Today filling up with greenhouse, cold-frame and dahlia jobs I can't do;
  - losing my garden because it only lives in this browser (Settings literally warns me about this);
  - the free answer from ChatGPT being just as good.

## Would I keep using it?
In a week: yes. Today told me exactly what to do this weekend: broad beans, garlic and onions, each with depth, spacing and a four-step How-to. The garlic sheet's "from a garden centre, not the supermarket" is the kind of tip I'd never have known. In a month, only if it handles the bit before planting. On the app's plan I have three neat beds full of crops. In my real garden I have grass. Nothing in setup asked whether those beds exist. Today says "Garlic in Bed" and "Weed the beds (Bed and Bed)" as if they do, and the shopping list has no cardboard or compost for making them. I only found the cardboard method because I searched "dig up lawn", which works now. A real beginner would buy garlic, walk outside, and stand on the lawn wondering where "Bed" is. In November Today is mostly generic jobs ("Plant bare-root trees", "Clean and oil your tools", greenhouse vents I don't have), so I'd drift off.

## My top five changes
1. **Ask "Are these beds there yet?" in setup**, or add a "Lawn, no beds yet" option. Mark the kit's beds as "to make". Make Today's first job "Make these beds this weekend (cardboard and compost)", with a How-to, and put "Cardboard: about N boxes" and "Compost: about N bags" per bed on the shopping list. Until a bed is made, its jobs should read "once Bed 1 is made". Then check the existing "all lawn" job actually appears: I couldn't trigger it.
2. **Never leave a kit's plants homeless.** With the border off, put the bulbs in two pots by the back door, or crocus in the lawn, and drop "along the border" from the kit's description.
3. **Fix the plan labels:** one tidy label per bed ("Bed 1: garlic, onion"), no overlapping row labels, and beds numbered when they're made. Show "row 1 of 2 · 20" on a picked row, so the plan and the shopping list agree at a glance.
4. **Make the shopping list shoppable:**
   - an amount on every line;
   - split it into "Buy now" and "Buy by February/March";
   - one general feed instead of five;
   - a short "where" line (garden centre, online, pound shop for pots).
5. **Clean up the plan's leftovers and units:**
   - Make "Set this ground" a visible toggle ("Locked · Unlock") or remove it, and rewrite Tip 2 to match.
   - Use cm everywhere, with one spacing number shared by the list, the page and the How-to.
   - Hide general jobs for greenhouses and cold frames that aren't on the plan.
