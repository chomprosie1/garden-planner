# Review and plan: bugs, the user journey, and three testers (11 Oct 2026)

After release 30. Five reviewers used the running app at phone size (390 × 844, headless Edge), each with its own garden:

- **A bug hunter**, reading releases 25 to 30 and confirming suspicions with throwaway tests.
- **A UX designer**, walking every main journey and looking at where each thing is shown.
- **Priya, 34**, a millennial gardener in Bristol: two raised beds, a dozen pots, two minutes at a time on her phone.
- **Margaret, 72**, a "technogran" in Shropshire: fifty years of gardening, veg plot, greenhouse and cold frame, iPad, eyes not what they were.
- **Jordan, 27**, a first-time gardener in Leeds: at home with polished apps, knows nothing about gardening.

All four testers started in **list mode** (it said "Simplest"); the UX designer and Jordan also tried a drawn plan. Nobody could draw on the canvas through the test driver, so drawing itself wasn't tested. Changes sometimes didn't survive between runs because the test browser was killed before it saved: that's the test rig, not the app.

---

## What everyone agreed on

Five things came up from three or more reviewers, unprompted. These matter most.

1. **The app contradicts itself about timing.** Today says "Sow broad beans tomorrow"; the same planting's card says "Next: sow outside Feb–Apr". Onions the same ("Mar–Apr" against "plant autumn sets now"). Every reviewer hit it. It goes to the heart of the app's promise. *Cause found:* `nextStep` in [src/ui/Inspector.tsx](../src/ui/Inspector.tsx) only reads a plant's **first** sowing window, so broad beans' Oct–Nov window is never shown.
2. **Every job in "This week" says TOMORROW.** On a Sunday with time free, all six said tomorrow. Planned plantings are given a date of the first day after today; `dayWords` in [src/calendar/week.ts](../src/calendar/week.ts) then says "tomorrow" truthfully about a made-up date. "Seedlings up soon" also shows for things not yet sown, and for onion sets, which never make seedlings.
3. **The shopping list uses the wrong words, and would send people to buy the wrong thing.** "Garlic seed", "Onion seed" (for sets), "Potato seed", and "Crocus, 324 plants". [src/planting/shopping.ts](../src/planting/shopping.ts) calls everything that isn't bought as a plant "seed". Margaret: "True potato seed is a different thing altogether." Jordan: "A beginner would follow this list and waste the season."
4. **Doing something in one place doesn't update the others.** Sowing sweet peas in Seedlings leaves "Sow sweet pea" unticked on Today, the seed tin still says "about 20 seeds", and the seed stays on the shopping list.
5. **Today buries the jobs.** The photo takes the top 55% of the screen; "This week" scrolls sideways (Margaret "would never have found jobs 3 to 6"); the same jobs appear again as "This month" with tick boxes 1,400 px further down; up to 15 cards stack below.

Also from more than one: toasts squashed to one word a line; Save greyed out on "Sown or planted it?" until you tap the only choice; plan wording in list mode ("on the plan", "Drag to move… Arrow keys nudge"); the Seedlings tab also being the seed tin and the feed shelf, while toasts say "in the shed"; Next year and the shopping list only found by accident under "All October jobs"; place search finding Leeds, Alabama.

## What worked (keep it)

- **Setup in under two minutes**, four plain steps, with a kit that gives real October jobs from the first minute. Priya: "a first for one of these apps."
- **The writing.** Plain, warm, specific ("2.5 cm deep, pointed end up"). Margaret checked onions, garlic, leeks, potatoes and broad beans: almost all sound. "Companion planting is partly garden lore" earned trust.
- **The plant pages** (calendar, kinds, pests, neighbours, recipes). Jordan: "This is where the app teaches best."
- **The seed tin, feed shelf, journal and harvest log**: "exactly what my notebook does" (Margaret). The shopping list taking off what's in the tin.
- **List mode** is calm and readable, and the planting sheet is the same in both modes.
- **Four dock tabs** is the right number.

### Smaller

- **Sowing doesn't close the matching job.** It also doesn't take seeds off the tin or the item off the shopping list.
- **The "Sown or planted it?" form** keeps Save greyed out until you tap the only pill.
- **Toasts** are about 190 px wide, so they wrap one word a line.
- **The toast and the bed card disagree:** "It's full now" sits beside "Plenty of room".
- **The onion job** ends in a stray fragment ("…A week or two before planting.").
- **The bulb job repeats itself:** "A small handful in the hole. A sprinkle in the bottom of the hole."
- **Unnamed beds in list mode** are all "Bed" until you switch view. The next-year picker then shows duplicates.
- **"Growing in Bed 2 (planned) and Bed 2 (planned)"**, from two rows.
- **Plan-only search commands** do nothing in list mode.
- **A bed dialog can stick invisible** after Ctrl+Z.
- **Place search:**
  - It returns US towns.
  - Enter doesn't search.
  - Pressing Next without picking keeps "the middle of England" silently.
  - A stray bullet shows beside the result.
- **"Dates are UK averages; adjust for your area"** shows after a postcode is set.
- **Units are mixed:** "1.20 m × 600 mm".
- **Plan labels and nudge bubbles overlap** at whole-garden zoom.
- **The month letters** under the year slider run together.

**Not a bug:** the UX designer thought the daily welcome returned the same day. The code records the day as soon as it shows (`Welcome.tsx:33`); it was the test browser being killed before it saved.

**Checked and sound:**
- **Migrations 17 to 20, validation of the new fields, and export and import.**
- **The rotation's bed mapping and the RHS order.**
- **Dates:** 29 Feb, the August flip, and local-day dates.
- **The watering job.**
- **The straighten and edge-length maths.**
- **Undo across the list/plan switch.**

## The testers in a line each

- **Priya** would keep it "for the Monday jobs and the seed tin". She'd drift back to Notion by week two if ticking and sowing don't change Today and the ⚠ on her tomato pot never goes. She wants a share image she'd actually post: the scale plan with a grey house isn't it.
- **Margaret** wouldn't give up the notebook yet. She'd run both side by side this winter. "When it calls garlic seed and says my broad beans are both now and February, I start checking everything." She wants bigger text, darker captions, the proper words, and the nearest real weather station.

## The plan

Five releases, cheapest and most trust-restoring first. Each is a normal release (tests, build, "as built", What's new, push). The schedule is in the build plan, under "The sixth round".

### Release 31: One answer everywhere (trust)

Fix what makes the app contradict itself or tell people to buy the wrong thing. All small; together they matter most.

1. **The "Next" line reads every sowing window** and says the one open now or next ("Sow outside now, until Nov"; "Next: sow Feb–Apr"). Never shows a past window as next. A test that the planting's line and the month's jobs agree for autumn sowings (broad bean, garlic, onion sets, sweet pea). (B8)
2. **Real days, not "tomorrow".** A job whose window is open says **Now** or **This week**; weekday words only for dates that come from something recorded. No "Seedlings up soon" until a sowing is recorded, and never for sets, cloves or bulbs. (B9)
3. **The shopping list says what you buy.** Seed, **seed potatoes**, **onion sets**, **garlic cloves**, **bulbs**, plants, from how each plant is started. Counts of bulbs as bulbs. Leave off anything already sown or planted. A short packet ("3 seeds left") doesn't cancel the line; ticks reset with the year. (Shopping part of B15)
4. **"Already in the ground" asks roughly when** (this spring / this summer / last autumn / a date), so the advice fits the season and next year's draft sees it. (B10)
5. **The autumn kit uses autumn-sown kinds**; autumn broad beans' days to harvest corrected. (B13)
6. **Plant, not sow, for cloves, sets and bulbs**: "Plant garlic", "Plant tulip bulbs". "Plant out" kept for seedlings.
7. **Words left in by mistake:** take out the 14 "(check)"s; fix the onion and bulb job texts; "Sow under cover now" stops saying "nothing" above sweet peas. (B14, B16)
8. **Sowing closes its job**, takes the seeds off the tin's count, and takes the item off the shopping list.
9. **Save works straight away** on "Sown or planted it?" (the next stage chosen for you), and one name for recording a step everywhere: "Mark as sown", then "Mark as up", and so on.
10. **Toasts as wide as the screen** (less a margin), buttons inline.

Tests: the planting line and the jobs agree; shopping kinds and counts; the "already growing" date reaches the draft; sowing ticks the job and spends the seed. Run the full suite (it touches `calendar/jobs`, `lifecycle/projection`, `data/plants`).

### Release 32: List mode, put right

List mode is how all four testers chose to start, so its bugs come next.

1. **New beds stay inside the garden** and never redraw its outline. If a drawn garden's full, say so and offer to put it to one side, rather than silently growing the boundary. (B1)
2. **No stacking.** More plants than fit: plant what fits and say "Room for 40 of 60: put the rest in another bed?" The same for planting out a big tray. (B2)
3. **Planters behave like beds** for rows and fill; only round pots are "pots". (B3)
4. **One plant in a pot is never over-full**, and a pot's room is its round area against the plant's spread (not a square of its spacing). Pots and planters do say when they're full. (B4, and the pot part of B9 in the bug list)
5. **Climbers show in the list**, under the fence or arch they grow on. (B5)
6. **Moving a row shortens it** to what fits the new bed. Fill keeps inside round and L-shaped beds. (B6, B7)
7. **"How many?" when adding pots** (Priya's twelve), and size in "Name, sun and soil". Duplicate in the bed's ··· menu.
8. **Number beds as they're added** ("Bed 1", "Bed 2"), not only when switching view.
9. **No plan wording in list mode**: "on the plan", "Drag to move… Arrow keys nudge", "Bring to front", the cardboard and ground help. Plan-only search commands hidden in list mode.
10. **Rows of the same plant fold into one line** ("Onion × 180, 2 rows").
11. **A ··· menu for the whole list** (Next year, Shopping list, switch to a plan), matching the plan's.

Tests: list placement (inside the garden, no stacking, planters, pots), fullness, moves. Run the full suite (`planting/place`).

### Release 33: Today, and where things live

The UX designer's main recommendations, with the testers behind each.

1. **The top of Today is one list you can tick.**
   - The photo shrinks to a band of about 160 px, carrying the month's line.
   - "This week" becomes a vertical list with ticks: **Now**, then **Later this month**, then "All October jobs".
   - At most two situational cards follow (frost, ready for the garden).
   - Install, What's new and the Your garden card go below the fold.
   - Backup is asked about once, not three times.
2. **The month's line fits the garden.** Pick the lines that mention what's grown, so nobody is told to lift dahlias they don't have.
3. **Planning ahead on Today from September to February**: one card with Shopping list · Next year.
4. **Seedlings becomes Shed.**
   - The tab's own tabs become Trays · Seed tin · Feed shelf.
   - Toasts name the actual place: "in the cold frame", "on the windowsill".
5. **One Settings screen.** It holds:
   - **This garden:** place, north, soil, frosts, list or plan.
   - **Reminders.**
   - **Backups.**
   - **Look and text size**, with text size near the top and a real Extra large.
   - **Help and What's new.**

   There's one way in, the same icon on every screen. "Your garden" stays as the name of the first section.
6. **A calmer first landing.** Land on Today with the first job picked out, or on the plan with only the toast: nothing pre-selected, and no tip until the first tap.
7. **Legibility.**
   - Darken the small grey captions, and check them in `tests/contrast.test.ts`.
   - Add a key to the year bars.
   - The year slider's month letters become seasons when it's narrow.
   - Bed labels and job chips give way to a count per bed at whole-garden zoom.
8. **Place search keeps to the UK.**
   - Partial postcodes work.
   - Enter searches.
   - Next with results showing asks you to pick one.
   - The stray bullet goes.
9. **"Dates are UK averages"** only shows when no place is set.

Tests: `words`, `contrast`, `ux-daily`, `ux-first-minute`. Screenshots at 390 px of Today, the first landing, Settings and the Shed.

### Release 34: Explain the gardening

For Jordan, and anyone new.

1. **A glossary of about 40 words**, in `src/content/`: two plain lines each.
   - Start from Jordan's list: hardening off, under cover, module tray, root trainer, propagator, pinch out, side-shoot, truss, cordon, plant out, sets, cloves, overwintering, mulch, well-rotted manure, green manure, leaf mould, lift, bare-root, fleece, cold frame, blanch, germinate, staking, crop family, rotation, succession sowing, earthing up, pricking out, potting on, chit, thin out, bolting, blood fish and bone, bonemeal, and variety (with Aquadulce as the example).
2. **Words in jobs and plant pages are tappable** where they're glossary words: a dotted underline opens the two lines. The glossary words are searchable from search.
3. **Each job opens a "how to" first:**
   - what you need;
   - how to do it, in three or four steps;
   - about how long it takes;
   - a **Done** button.

   The planting's details sit underneath. Most of the words are already in the jobs and plant data, so this is mainly layout.
4. **Kits sized for a first garden.**
   - Tens of bulbs, not hundreds.
   - A "Make it bigger" option.
   - Kits that promise something do it ("broad beans in the other bed").
5. **A not-now note on plant pages out of season**: "Tomatoes go in from mid-May; sow indoors from February."
6. **A warning when a bed is booked.** Adding to a bed already taken until August says so.
7. **Help gaps:** short topics on This week's jobs, Recording a harvest, and Feeding, plus a list-mode version of cardboard over weeds. Help also gets a **?** in the header on every tab.

Tests: `words`; a test that every glossary word used in data has an entry.

### Release 35: Next year, and the seasons, done properly

1. **Overwintering crops belong to the year they're harvested.** Garlic in October 2026 is a 2027 crop, and its bed is busy until it's lifted. Fullness adds what's in now to what's planned. (B12)
2. **Using the draft twice replaces the first use**, so it doesn't add to it. (B11)
3. **The draft lists every bed.**
   - It highlights the bed on the small plan.
   - It explains why a crop stays put, or is left out ("not sown yet", "no date").
   - Patches of ground rotate too.
4. **"What did you grow this summer?"** A quick way to tell an empty draft which crops were where, so the first year isn't wasted (Priya).
5. **Autumn and early sowings filled in.**
   - Hardy peas in Oct–Nov.
   - Winter salads under glass.
   - Broad beans in root trainers.
   - Hardy annuals.
   - Sweet peas in a cold frame, rather than "indoors".
6. **Season-aware advice for plants already growing.** No fleece or weekly feeding for tomatoes in October.
7. **Climate check (data).**
   - Check the nearest-station choice: Shawbury for SY5, not Aberporth.
   - Check the "days above 5 °C" sum: 336 to 365 days is too long for the UK.
   - Say frost dates as "50:50; play safe until…".

Tests: `next-year`, `jobs`, `stages`, `microclimate`, `warmth`, then the full suite.

### Parked, with reasons

These were raised but aren't worth doing before the five releases above.

- **A share picture worth posting** (Priya): an illustrated "my beds this month" card. It's worth doing, but the trust fixes come first, so it goes on the wishlist (M).
- **3D on a phone** opens on the house wall, and "standing in it" was grey (possibly headless). It's a nice extra, so: check once on a real phone, and if it's still poor there, open 3D facing the beds (S).
- **Wrapped popping up** after the first harvest of the year ("2 plantings, of 1 crop"). Hold it until there's a season's worth (S). This could go in release 33.
- **Costs on the shopping list** (Jordan). Prices were taken out in release 29 because they go out of date. Stay with that.
- **Simple/Advanced** moves into the ··· menu as part of release 33's plan tidy, rather than being a release of its own.

## How we'll know it worked

After release 33, ask your two testers to do the same three things the personas did:
1. Set up a garden from scratch.
2. Say what to do this week.
3. Draft next year.

Count the times they hit a contradiction or a word they didn't know. Today's baseline from this review:
- every tester hit the timing contradiction;
- three of the four hit the shopping-list words;
- Jordan found about 35 unexplained terms.
