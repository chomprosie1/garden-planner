## Would I pay for it?
**Maybe, which is new.** £10 to £15 a year, or £15 once. That's on top of Planta, not instead of it: it doesn't do houseplants, plant ID or "what's wrong with it?". Before I paid, watering needs to change with the weather and the season, and the greenhouse lines need to go.

## What I most wanted to know
**The balcony questions: yes.** Which way it faces, how much sun, a roof above, and windy, on the same screen as choosing Balcony. They carry through: every container says "part shade", and a backup keeps them.

**The kits:** "Shady balcony" is parsley, rocket and winter purslane in the trough, with mint and alpine strawberries in pots. That's what I asked for last time.

**Watering when it's covered and windy: mostly.** Today now starts:

> **Water: Chilli pot (25 cm across, about 15 litres).** Rain doesn't reach it here. Wind dries a pot fast. The smallest dries fastest. Push a finger into the compost first: if it's moist below the surface, leave it for now.

That's the job I wanted, and it's in the list view. The finger test is honest. Two limits:
- It's the same on 30 October, 15 January and 15 June. With the weather on, the words didn't change at all. It doesn't know a hot week from a cold one.
- A chilli I'm told to bring indoors is still on the balcony's watering job.

**What's offered for each pot: better, with one odd habit.** Add a plant says "Plants that suit Salad trough" and no butterfly bushes. Easy to start gives Lettuce and Swiss chard; Sow or plant now gives corn salad, pansies, primrose. But the suggested swaps are odd: basil in part shade gets "These would suit it: Daffodil, Corn salad, English bluebell", and strawberries get "Daffodil, Foxglove, Aquilegia". I wanted something to eat. And the same list comes up for a 25 cm chilli pot as for a 60 cm trough.

**The Seedlings tab:** it's called Seedlings for a balcony. Good. It still has "Add a place", which reads oddly for a windowsill.

**Would it replace anything I pay for?** No. It sits beside Planta.

**A copy off my phone:** Keep a copy on Today made `balcony-4b-2026-10-30.json`, and the line changed to "Last copy today". A fresh browser restored it, balcony answers and all. I'd trust the file. I'd want to know where it's gone on my phone.

## What I like (keep it)
- **The Add a bed presets:** 30 cm pot, 60 cm trough, Grow bag, Strawberry tower. Sizes in cm, and a tower holds its 8 plants ("Nearly full").
- **No rotation, soil picker or weeding** in Settings or Today for a balcony.
- **"Bring indoors: Chilli pepper… Before nights drop to about 12 °C… Fleece won't keep it warm enough."** The fleece job I complained about has gone from that plant.
- **The shopping list gives weight:** "about 300 kg once it's wet". That's the number my letting agent would ask about.
- **A chosen reminder day.** It's a drop-down, Monday to Sunday.
- **The warnings are grouped:** "Chilli pot: chilli pepper wants a pot about 30 cm across; chilli pepper wants full sun, and this is part shade."
- **It opens on Today.** The pumpkin splash has gone.

## What I don't like
Worst first.
1. **Watering doesn't change with the weather or the season.** It's the same advice in January as in June, weather on or off. Planta at least changes its schedule. In winter a weekly nag about pots under a roof will teach me to ignore it.
2. **Greenhouse and cold-frame words are still there.** The Sow job: "For leaves all winter: sow in modules, then plant into a greenhouse border or cold frame." November's Around the garden: "Protect tender plants with fleece". I told it Balcony.
3. **Daffodils instead of basil.** The swaps ignore why I wanted the plant.
4. **300 kg of compost, and no warning.** The list says so, but nothing says that's a lot for a balcony with a load limit, or suggests fewer pots. The kit's 50 cm pot for mint is a big part of it.
5. **A plant typed into a 35 cm pot is "too crowded" though the tower preset is 35 cm too.** My pot "Strawberry tower" (typed as 35 cm) said "Over-full" and "It's full now" with 8 plants. The preset tower is also 35 cm and says "Nearly full". Same size, different answer, because I named mine myself.
6. **Ferns still don't come up under Shade.** "3 of 251 plants": bluebell, hellebore, hosta. The number's now honest, but a fern is the obvious answer for shade, and you have to search for it.
7. **"Plant out: Winter purslane in Planter"** in November. It's a trough I said I'd sow into.
8. **Today's first job in a kit is sowing into modules** for something I could sow straight into the trough.
