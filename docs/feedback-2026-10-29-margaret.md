## Would I pay for it?
**Maybe.** I'd pay £15 to £20 as a one-off, or about £10 a year. I wouldn't pay monthly: I'd forget, then resent it. For comparison, Gardeners' World magazine is about £60 a year, my RHS membership is about £80 and the seed catalogues are free. This app would have to earn a place beside those, not replace them.

Before I paid:
- Today must never ask me to sow or plant something I've already recorded as sown or planted.
- A planting must take a variety ('Aquadulce Claudia', 'Musselburgh', 'Radar').
- There must be a page listing what I've picked this year.

What would make me cancel: wrong advice I have to catch myself, or losing my garden. "Kept only in this browser" worries me. If the iPad went, the whole year would go with it.

## Would I keep using it?
Probably, yes, this winter at least. Setting up was the best it's been: the text size is at the top of Settings, my bed sizes stuck, the fruit cage and the "Its crops move round the beds" switch are exactly what I asked for, and "Already in the ground" now asks what stage a plant is at. The sweet peas say June and the raspberries aren't flowering. The rotation draft is something I'd actually use, once I'd tidied its wording. But the first thing Today showed me was a list telling me to sow the sweet peas, sow the broad beans, and plant the garlic and onions, all of which I'd just entered as in. I had to tick them off a second time. That's exactly the double work a notebook doesn't make me do. And the parsnips "finished by about 10 Dec" made me go back to checking the dates.

## What I like (keep it)

- **Bed sizes stick** (Garden › ··· › Name, size, sun and soil). I typed 3 m, changed the sun, pressed Done, and it stayed 3 m. "Another like this" no longer renames the original.
- **Kinds of bed** (Add a bed): greenhouse, cold frame and fruit cage each have a sensible default size. My 6 × 8 ft greenhouse came up as 1.8 × 2.4 m without my asking. Sizes read "1.2 × 0.6 m" now, not mixed units.
- **"Its crops move round the beds each year"** is on for the veg beds and off for the border. That's the right default, and it's explained in one line.
- **"Already in the ground" asks the stage**, with stages that fit the plant: garlic "Planted", parsnip "Sown / Up / Growing / Harvesting" (no planting out), leek "Planted out". Parsnips are now "Ready to lift", and their How to says to use a fork.
- **Sweet peas in the cold frame**: "Next: plant out from about 1 Apr", "In flower from about 6 Jun", and the frame is "about 4 °C warmer than outside by day". All right.
- **The How to cards** (Today › How to). Garlic: "keeping the papery skins on… 15 cm apart, in rows 30 cm apart". Sweet pea: root trainers in the cold frame, "No heat", "Keep mice out". Broad beans: "a few holly leaves over them". Parsnip: "Push the fork in beside the row… leave the rest in the ground, where they keep". That's what I'd say.
- **Shed › Sow seeds** offers the cold frame and greenhouse under "Where", and defaults sweet peas to root trainers.
- **All October jobs › Around the garden** now has the jobs I asked for: cut out fruited raspberry canes, clean the greenhouse glass and put up bubble wrap, keep the frame lid open by day. The dahlia job has gone from my list.
- **Next year's draft** (Today › Next year › Draft it). It leaves the fruit cage, border and greenhouse alone. It says Bed 2 is "Taken until about June by broad bean… already in for 2027" and suggests "follow on with kale, purple sprouting broccoli or cabbage". That's exactly how I'd plan it.
- **The garden words.** "Tender" now says "runner and French beans… (broad beans are hardy)", and "Variety" uses Aquadulce as its example. I'd hand the list to a beginner.
- **The shopping list** is short and clear: "Seed potatoes, first early, about 8 · For Bed 1".
- **Reminders' checkboxes** are big now and sit beside their labels.

## What I don't like
Worst first.

1. **Today asks me to do what I've already done.** I added garlic and onion sets as "Already in the ground › This month › Planted", broad beans as "Sown" (and then recorded 20 Oct), and sweet peas as "Sown" in the cold frame. Today's Now list still said "Sow under cover: Sweet pea", "Sow outside: Broad bean", "Plant: Garlic" and "Plant: Onion", and each bed card carried a "Plant: Garlic" or "Sow under cover: Sweet pea" chip. Under the broad bean job it also said "Seedlings up soon: broad bean". If it knows they're coming up, it knows they're sown.
2. **"Done" on a How to card ticks the job.** I opened How to for each job to check the advice, and pressed Done to close it. That ticked them all, including "Ready to lift: Parsnip" (6 of 7 done). I hadn't lifted anything. "Done" reads as "close" to me. Call it "I've done this" and give it a separate close button.
3. **Parsnips "Finished by about 10 Dec".** The same card says "Harvest Oct–Mar" and "The rest keeps in the ground", and next year's draft says they're "standing until about April". Parsnips stand all winter. If I believed the 10 Dec date, I'd lift the lot and lose half of them in the shed.
4. **Still no variety on a planting, and you can't search by one.** "Aquadulce" and "Radar" both give "No plants match.", in a dialog squeezed so narrow it breaks into two lines. I had to write the variety in a note.
5. **The fruit cage is "About half full" but full.** It took 8 raspberries and 2 blackcurrants. The third blackcurrant and both gooseberries got "no room left". It then offered me the Fruit cage again as somewhere to put them, and refused a second time.
6. **"6 ft" became 6 metres.** I typed "6 ft" and "8ft" for the greenhouse. It made it "8 × 6 m", the size of a bungalow, without a word. Either understand feet or refuse them.
7. **Harvests go nowhere I can find.** I recorded 1400 g of leeks with a note. The note shows in the journal; the weight only turns up on card 3 of "Your season, wrapped". There's no list of what I've picked, it only takes grams (no lb, and no "4 leeks"), and leeks don't appear under "Ready to lift" on Today even after I'd started picking them.
8. **The sweet pea warning contradicts its own card.** The card says "Plants 24, Row length 1.15 m, Spacing 230 mm". The warning says "50 mm apart; they need about 230 mm. Fewer plants, or a longer row". They're in root trainers in a frame. Pots aren't spaced at their final distance.
9. **Drop-downs and radio buttons don't grow with the text.** "It went in" and "It's now" in Add a plant, the "What is it?" radios in Add a bed, and "Takes the crops from" and "What grew here in 2026?" in Next year are all small. These are exactly the controls that matter.
10. **The plan on an iPad** is still the weak part. Labels pile on top of each other ("Bed Bed 4 onions and potatoes", "Long border" over "Bed 2 legumes"). The month letters under the slider still run together. The tip says "Click anything" and "Scroll to zoom… Press ? for all the shortcuts". The greenhouse sits outside the garden. "Small bed added" stays on screen after Undo.

## What's superfluous
- **"Your season, wrapped"** (Today › ···). It's the only place my harvest weight shows. Put the numbers on a plain "Harvests" page and drop the cards.
- **"Install the app" card** on Today, every visit, and **"Getting started"** once the beds are planted. "Download a backup" deserves a line in Settings, not a checklist.
- **"Inspire me" / "Room to grow"** on every empty bed and the greenhouse. I know what I'm growing.
- **Five "Looks"**, "Light or dark" and "More display options" in Settings. One look and the text size is all I need.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** in Settings, and "press Ctrl+K" in Help, on an iPad.
- **3D, In flower / Harvest / Water and "Play the year"** on the plan. I'd never use them, and the slider's months can't be read.
- **"Keep apart from…" companion lists** on every vegetable. Honest that it's lore, but it's still clutter.
- **Recipes on the plant pages.** Pleasant, but four recipe tiles push "Look out for" and the pests a long way down.
- **The note under the jobs list**: "Harvest and winter jobs start once a planting has a sowing date. Ticking its sowing job sets the date…". It reads like instructions to a programmer. If the app needs a date, it should ask.

## What's missing
- **A variety on each planting**, shown on the bed card ("Broad bean 'Aquadulce Claudia' × 30"), and found by search.
- **A harvest record**: a list by crop and date, in lb and oz or kg, or as a count ("4 leeks"), with a year's total.
- **Feet and inches** in bed sizes, shown beside metres.
- **A weather note that counts.** My journal note says "Frost on the frame lid, −2 on the max-min". The app should offer to set my first frost from that, and remember it for next year.
- **Last year on this date**, and a search through the journal. It's what I use the notebook for most.
- **A "Resting" stage for fruit**, and winter pruning jobs for the blackcurrants and gooseberries.
- **Room for five fruit bushes and a raspberry row** in a 4 × 3 m cage, which is what mine holds.
- **A greenhouse's warmth** (unheated, frost-free, heated) in its settings, so the winter salads are timed right.
- **A backup that isn't only on this iPad.** Offer to send it to my email or iCloud each month.

## My top five changes
1. **Make a planting's stage the one source for Today.** If a planting is recorded as sown or planted (by "Already in the ground", "What's happened?" or the Shed), its sow or plant job and the bed's chip go away. Rename the How to sheet's "Done" to "I've done this" and add a plain close button.
2. **Give a planting a variety**: a field on Add a plant and on the card ("Broad bean 'Aquadulce Claudia' × 30"), searchable from Add a plant and Plants, and carried into the shopping list and next year's draft.
3. **Add a Harvests page**: every pick by crop and date, in lb and oz or kg, or as a count, with a total for the year. Put it in the Garden menu, not in "Season, wrapped". Show leeks and other standing crops under "Ready to lift" while they're being picked.
4. **Fix the winter crops' end dates and the onion page.** Parsnips, leeks and other standing roots finish in March or April, not December. The overwintering onion page needs one story: hardy in the ground all winter, sets September to November, a short keeper. White rot should read the same on the garlic and onion pages. Add a "Resting" stage for fruit, with winter pruning jobs for blackcurrants and gooseberries.
5. **Make sizes and spacing behave like a gardener's.** Accept feet ("6 ft", "6'") or refuse them clearly. Pack a fruit cage so a raspberry row and five bushes fit 4 × 3 m. Don't spacing-check pots and root trainers in a frame or greenhouse. And make every drop-down and radio button grow with Extra large.
